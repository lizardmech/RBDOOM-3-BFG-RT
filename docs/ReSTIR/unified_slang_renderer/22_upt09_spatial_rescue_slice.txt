UPT-09 Bounded Spatial Rescue Baseline
======================================

Status
------

Built and deployed as an opt-in Vulkan direct-reuse pass. The accepted
sub-policy defaults are unique-neighbor proof mode 0, direct proposal parity,
RTXDI target-PDF parity, analytic portal admission, UPT-08 duplication control,
and temporal history M/age 32. Temporal and spatial themselves remain default
off, and indirect/reconnection spatial shifting remains unimplemented. The
reciprocal-neighbor cost experiment and final unified-path quality/timing gates
remain open.

Why this baseline exists now
----------------------------

The first real-map UPT-07 runs proved that temporal reprojection was often
surface-compatible while the exact history receiver held no selected direct
sample. View 5 therefore became almost solid rejection reason 2. Searching
other temporal taps for a selected sample was rejected: it mixed a favorable
spatial search into temporal ownership and previously caused watery motion,
bright persistence, and trails.

A temporary portal-selected analytic-light restriction improved small portal
rooms but failed immediately in larger areas. In one large-area diagnostic it
issued about 2.82 million selected visibility rays yet retained only about
156,000 positive direct reservoirs, losing roughly 94.5 percent. That A/B was
reverted. The full eligible light universe remains visible to D0; spatial
rescue must expose rather than hide proposal-domain failure.

Admitted GPU work
-----------------

Pass:

    UPT.S0 Spatial Direct Rescue

Inputs:

    current canonical 176-byte primary surfaces
    completed current 64-byte reservoir page from D0 or T0
    current direct-light stream, either legacy 112-byte or compact 64-byte
    the existing emissive replay and geometry route streams
    live TLAS

Output:

    the other already-admitted 64-byte reservoir page

There is no third full-frame page, per-frame clear, material table, sampler,
bindless texture table, diagnostic side buffer, or CPU readback. Both output
pages are fully covered; invalid receivers and unrecovered receivers receive
self-validating canonical empty records.

The compute group is 8 x 8. A selected center attempts three neighbors; an
empty center attempts twelve. Coordinates come from a fixed 32-point disc with
a 30-pixel radius and a frame/pixel/ordinal hash. The compile-time maximum is
twelve attempts. Surface and reservoir validity plus surface class, normal,
roughness, depth, and receiver-plane compatibility are checked before direct
sample shift/replay.

Only the final selected borrowed sample may issue visibility, so the shader has
one RayQuery site and the per-pixel ceiling is one visibility ray. Analytic and
emissive direct events use exact current-light replay. Invalid or unsupported
selected neighbors do not increase M. A structurally valid count-only neighbor
does increase M and participates in the bias-correction denominator: it is a
defined zero trial published by D0, not an invalid sample. It can never become
the selected identity or issue a ray. A valid receiver with an empty center is
allowed to search and can recover a neighbor sample.

This first runtime lane is explicitly direct-only. Enabling the CVar while the
unified or indirect-only family is selected does not dispatch S0, because the
baseline does not yet preserve and shift indirect events. Partial support must
not discard a valid indirect center.

Two-page schedule
-----------------

The physical roles are deliberately not copied or cleared:

    current role <- D0 initial
    current role <- optional T0 temporal, in place
    full UAV/SRV transition
    history role <- S0(current role)
    R0 reads history role
    history role remains next-frame temporal history

When S0 completes, CompleteFrame does not swap roles. On the next frame D0
overwrites the current role while T0 reads the spatial result in the history
role. If S0 is disabled or fails after a successful T0, the established
temporal-only role swap remains in force. Resolve chooses the spatial output
only after S0 has actually completed.

----------------------

The verification target proves both variants expose exactly nineteen narrow

Runtime control and first gate
------------------------------

Enable only after the normal direct-only UPT-07 setup is producing the current
diagnostic result:

    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtSpatial 1

Keep:

    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtFamily 1
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtBackend 0
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtProofStage 9
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtShaderProof 6
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtResolve 1
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtTemporal 1

First compare resolve view 5 with spatial off and on in the same large area.
The baseline must materially replace reason-2 red with accepted selected
reservoirs without bright flashes, trails, thin-wall leaks, or camera-motion
smearing. Then capture S0 timing, ray-query dependency, L2/VRAM throughput, and
active-thread coherence. An image improvement that merely hides D0's poor
visible-sample ratio is evidence for proposal work, not acceptance of S0.

Deferred work
-------------

The planned first production policy is reciprocal neighbor selection. This
random-disc implementation is only the required comparison baseline. Do not
declare UPT-09 complete until reciprocal selection either demonstrates its
intended cost reduction or is measured and rejected.

UPT-08 is a prerequisite for accepting a temporal+spatial production loop.
The first UPT-09 slice remains useful for diagnosing empty-center recovery,
but it must not be promoted as a stable production default without correlation
control. ReSTIR PT Enhanced identifies persistent blobs, disks, and streaks as
copies of one random sample circulating through spatiotemporal reuse. The local
maximum-history-M tests reproduced the same control only as a global quality
loss. UPT-08 must therefore compare fused sample-ID publication with an
explicit FillSampleID pass, exclude invalid IDs, match a CPU count oracle, and
feed an adaptive temporal cap before final UPT-09 acceptance.

The first UPT-08 proof now exists behind
r_pathTracingUnifiedPtDuplication (accepted default 1). U0 publishes stable sample IDs,
U1 counts matching IDs in a 17x17 neighborhood, and next-frame T0 reduces its
history-M cap from maximumHistoryM toward one using the packed local score.
Markers are "UPT.U0 Fill SampleID", "UPT.U1 Compute Duplication17x17", and
"UPT.T0 Temporal Direct Shift AdaptiveDupCap" once matching history is usable.
This is correlation containment, not permission to retain incorrect shift,
target-density, visibility, or light-identity math.

Runtime acceptance after correcting the adaptive uncorrelated cap to 20 found
mode 0 stable through temporal maximum age 32. Age 63 could still amplify a few
isolated regions and provided no observed quality benefit. The production
default is therefore age 32; 63 remains available only as a saturation/no-expiry
stress case. Mode 3 remains a useful immune 1/M control but is visibly slightly
worse and is not selected merely to conceal the age-63 mode-0 failure.

First runtime result and correction
-----------------------------------

The bounded S0 baseline materially repaired direct-reservoir density, proving
that the empty-center rescue direction is useful.  It also made two pre-existing
T0/D0 light-contract regressions obvious: rigid Doom-radius lighting volumes
and temporal poisoning/strobing from animated lights.  S0 did not create either
rule, but its wider sample coverage propagated their visible consequences.

The correction is owned below S0.  Shared UPT analytic replay no longer applies
the legacy diagnostic hard radius mask, and T0 now admits history only for
manager-proven stable-cacheable analytics and non-dynamic emissives.  S0 may
still reuse those lights across current-frame neighbors; it must not invent a
second temporal-stability policy.  Runtime acceptance of the spatial baseline
remains open until the same large-area animated-light test is clean.

The subsequent runtime A/B confirmed the light identity/stability correction:
the animated-light strobe is gone.  It also showed a separate motion failure in
which the previous-view polygon can remain visible and valid history grows
outward over later frames.  T0 now rejects rigid/skinned receivers that lack
valid packed object motion and rejects mature history contribution outliers;
view 5 reports those cases as orange and pink.  Runtime testing found only a
modest tracking improvement: rigid flicker and portal-boundary instability both
remain, so neither is accepted as fixed.  S0 remains intentionally unchanged.
A remaining expanding red boundary is sparse D0 seed coverage, not permission
for S0 or T0 to widen their search until a positive sample is found.

Rejected rescue-only A/B after proposal parity
----------------------------------------------

A real-map cable test showed progressive brightening with the original 3/12
schedule. A rescue-only A/B changed selected-center attempts from three to zero
and empty-center attempts from twelve to four. It heavily degraded spatial
coverage and made the same regions darken instead of brighten. The A/B is
rejected and the 3/12 schedule is restored. Neighbor count is therefore not
the root correction; the remaining investigation belongs to spatial estimator
normalization and the D0 internal-RIS proposal contract.

Count-only neighbor normalization correction
--------------------------------------------

The follow-up audit found that S0 accepted only neighbors with selected
samples. This violated D0's self-describing zero-trial contract: a valid
count-only record carries effective M even though it has no selectable
identity. Excluding those records conditioned spatial reuse on a neighbor
having found illumination. That explains why the 3/12 schedule could brighten
sparse cable geometry while the reduced schedule darkened the same area.

S0 now admits a compatible count-only neighbor into output M and the
target-weighted bias-correction denominator. Its mass remains zero, so it
cannot win reservoir selection and cannot issue visibility. Malformed records
and selected records whose direct event cannot be replayed remain excluded.
This correction preserves the original 3/12 coverage schedule while removing
the positive-only neighborhood conditioning; runtime acceptance remains open.

Alpha-coverage reuse boundary
-----------------------------

The next real-map A/B isolated a second failure to alpha-clipped geometry:
cutout surfaces brightened repeatedly and contaminated samples could propagate
away from the card when no stronger geometric boundary stopped them. The
original S0 compatibility gate compared surface class, geometric normal,
roughness, depth, and receiver plane only. A visible texel on a cutout card and
the opaque surface visible through its neighboring rejected texel can satisfy
all of those tests, yet they are different sampling domains. Cross-domain
shifted weights can become extreme and are fed back through the temporal and
spatial pages on later frames.

The canonical 176-byte primary record already carries the material alpha-test
flag, stable material ID, and alpha cutoff. No buffer, descriptor, or dispatch
is justified for this correction. Shared surface compatibility now treats
alpha coverage as an explicit edge: if either surface is alpha-tested, both
must be alpha-tested, have the same stable material ID, and have the same
finite alpha cutoff. The dense material-table index is deliberately not used
because it is a frame-local address. Opaque-to-opaque reuse retains the broad
existing gate. Both T0 and S0 apply this rule so an invalid cutout crossing
cannot enter history through one pass and be propagated by the other.

Runtime acceptance must repeat the cable/card test with S0 off and on and
confirm that brightness no longer grows over time or travels beyond the
cutout silhouette. This guard does not implement alpha-aware visibility for
S0 RayQuery; that is a separate traversal/material ABI decision and must not
be inferred from this surface-reuse fix.

The first runtime test showed no improvement, disproving stable alpha-material
identity as the operative boundary in that scene. The primary producer can
composite filter/alpha layers into the evaluated receiver albedo while
deliberately retaining the underlying receiver identity. More generally, S0
had omitted the reference spatial material-similarity gate: it compared
roughness but ignored evaluated diffuse albedo and specular F0. S0 now applies
the FullSample thresholds to the canonical record (50 percent relative
roughness difference, 0.25 reflectivity-luminance difference, and 0.25
diffuse-luminance difference). This is the relevant guard for a composited
alpha edge that has no distinct material identity. Runtime acceptance remains
open; the no-improvement result is retained rather than overwritten.
Fault-isolation note (2026-08-05)
---------------------------------

Compatibility experiments on alpha identity and RTXDI-shaped material
similarity produced no visible change in the reported runaway-bright spatial
case.  Do not keep loosening or tightening surface gates on that evidence.

The next audit found a concrete estimator-input divergence: every UPT09
neighbor ordinal independently selected one of the 32 offset-table entries.
With 12 rescue attempts this made repeated table entries overwhelmingly likely,
and the same physical reservoir could be streamed and counted more than once.
Production mode now chooses one random start and walks distinct table entries;
it also rejects physical-pixel duplicates caused by viewport-edge clamping.

Use r_pathTracingUnifiedPtSpatialProofMode to isolate failures without changing
the resource graph or rebuilding shaders between cases:

  0 = production: unique neighbors, basic correction, non-recursive rescue
  1 = exact packed-reservoir pass-through through the S0 dispatch/page publish
  2 = reproduce the former recursive with-replacement rescue behavior
  3 = non-recursive unique neighbors, but standard streamed-weight 1/M normalization
  4 = strict legacy admission: selected center and selected neighbors only

Interpretation:

  - mode 1 differs from spatial-off: page ownership/resolve/history is wrong;
  - mode 0 is stable but mode 2 grows: duplicate reservoir admission caused it;
  - mode 0 grows but mode 3 is stable: the basic-correction denominator caused it;
  - modes 0 and 3 both grow while mode 1 is stable: the shifted stream or its
    recursively published mass is wrong, not surface compatibility.

Runtime result from the first proof build: mode 3 survived but progressively
darkened the region behind the alpha-clipped surface, while modes 0 and 2
retained the runaway failure.  This proves the basic-correction path preserves
the contamination while ordinary 1/M dilutes it with empty source domains; it
does not identify duplicate offsets or compatibility thresholds as the cause.

The exact legacy comparison then exposed the missing admission boundary:
legacy DI returns immediately for an invalid center and skips invalid neighbor
reservoirs.  UPT09 instead borrowed into an empty center and published that
borrowed winner as an ordinary reusable reservoir.  The rescue could therefore
donate again in later temporal/spatial frames.  Production now writes a direct-
only replayKey stamp into empty-center rescue winners.  Resolve may consume the
winner for the current frame, but UPT07 and UPT09 reject the stamp as history or
as a future donor.  This is stamp-and-skip invalidation with no extra buffer,
clear, ray, or per-pixel storage.  Mode 2 deliberately preserves the old
recursive behavior; mode 4 disables rescue entirely as the legacy control.

Runtime result after the replay stamp: modes 0 and 4 still exhibited the
runaway alpha-card failure. Mode 3 remained materially better, although it
could darken the region behind the card over time. This ruled out both repeated
rescue donation and empty-center admission as the sole root cause: mode 4 could
fail using only ordinarily valid selected reservoirs. Later target-measure,
identity, duplication, and bounded-history corrections made mode 0 stable at
the accepted age-32 setting. Mode 3 is retained as an immune 1/M diagnostic;
it is visibly worse and is not the production default.

Direct target-PDF parity experiment
-----------------------------------

The next comparison found a precise measure mismatch rather than another
surface threshold. Both the accepted legacy DI lane and RTXDI FullSample define
the direct reservoir target as:

    luminance(BRDF * incidentRadiance * cosine) / lightSample.solidAnglePdf

The discrete light-selection PDF and technique-MIS weight are applied by the
scalar reservoir stream. Original UPT instead stored max(R,G,B) of the
MIS-weighted contribution as its target, stored that same MIS-weighted RGB
contribution, and divided the candidate weight by selectionPdf *
solidAnglePdf. These forms can give the same initial one-sample estimate, but
they are not the same target measure. Basic correction re-evaluates the stored
target at multiple receiver surfaces, so the missing solid-angle term, the
max-channel norm, and the embedded technique-MIS term can produce radically
different cross-surface ratios even when initial D0 looks plausible.

r_pathTracingUnifiedPtDirectTargetPdfParity selects a coherent D0/T0/S0 A/B:

  0 = original UPT max-channel target with technique MIS embedded in the
      stored RGB sample and target;
  1 = RTXDI measure above, with technique MIS applied once in the scalar RIS
      weight and un-MISed contribution/solidAnglePdf stored for resolve.

The mode is part of the page content-generation fingerprint, so changing it
rejects incompatible history without clearing either 64-byte page. Never
change only S0's target evaluation: mixing measures between D0, T0, and S0
would make the proof invalid. Runtime acceptance is specifically mode 0 basic
correction with target-PDF parity 1 versus 0 on the same alpha-card view. Keep
mode 3 as the safe fallback if parity does not remove the growth.

Analytic portal-domain proof (2026-08-05)
-----------------------------------------

The target-PDF experiment did not remove the failure. Spatial proof mode 3
bounded its energy, but exposed a stronger clue: a tiny red analytic light with
little or no plausible influence over the receiver could dominate a large mesh.
That points at the admitted light domain before it points at another spatial
normalization formula.

The host intentionally publishes a globally stable analytic-light universe for
reservoir identity and previous-to-current remapping. D0, T0, and S0 were also
using that whole universe as the current receiver's proposal/replay domain.
Legacy Doom DI instead restricts analytic candidates to the current selected
portal region. The full light record already carries this classification in
uvOrDoomParams.z (negative outside the selected region), but the UPT 64-byte
compact-light route discarded it.

r_pathTracingUnifiedPtAnalyticPortalDomain provides one coherent D0/T0/S0 A/B:

  0 = retain the old behavior and admit the full global analytic range;
  1 = admit only analytic records selected by the current Doom portal region.

The global stable universe remains allocated and indexed in both cases. The
switch changes candidate and replay eligibility, not identity. Compact analytic
records preserve portal depth in the otherwise-unused packed normal word;
emissive records retain their octahedral normal. There is no ABI-size increase,
new buffer, clear, or ray.

This first proof deliberately samples the stable global range and turns an
outside-domain analytic draw into a null trial. That is statistically coherent
for the restricted domain but can reduce proposal density. If the domain proof
is accepted, a later host-built dense eligible-index distribution can recover
the wasted trials without changing reservoir identity.

Acceptance is unambiguous on the reported red-wall view:

  - red dominance disappears with mode 1: the omitted portal-domain boundary
    is confirmed;
  - red dominance remains with mode 1: the light is portal-selected, and the
    next audit is its Doom projected/falloff evaluation, not more normalization
    or compatibility guesses;
  - the red source disappears but coverage drops: correctness is confirmed and
    the next task is a compact eligible-index proposal distribution.

Do not replace this proof with a hard Doom-radius cutoff. The accepted legacy
lane deliberately allows smooth contribution beyond Doom's nominal influence
radius, and a hard cutoff recreates the rejected rigid light-volume boundary.

Runtime result: portal-domain mode 1 did not remove the failure. The dominant
light must be inside the same selected portal as the alpha-clipped receiver.
Mode 0 can lock onto several light colors; mode 3 bounds the growth for most
lights but a small red light can still dominate a large mesh. Portal membership
is therefore a necessary admission boundary, not the root cause. Keep the
boundary, but do not continue changing the light universe on this evidence.

Fresh source-target proof
-------------------------

The mode split exposed one remaining operation unique to basic correction.
The denominator replays the selected light at every participating surface, but
the numerator and neighbor mass conversion used the packed source target from
the donor reservoir. That packed value can have passed through earlier temporal
or spatial reuse. It is not proof of the light's target at the donor's current
surface. Reusing it combines a fresh denominator with a potentially stale
numerator; mode 3 bypasses that numerator entirely, matching its ability to
bound the failure.

Spatial proof mode 5 keeps mode 0's neighbors, selection, visibility, and basic
correction, but freshly replays each donor light at the actual donor surface for
both uses of the source target. It introduces no extra ray or resource. Compare
mode 5 directly against modes 0 and 3 on the red-light view:

  - mode 5 removes mode 0's growth: stale packed source targets caused the
    amplification; the fresh replay should replace the old path;
  - mode 5 matches mode 0: the source target is coherent, and the remaining
    failure is high-variance basic correction / correlated light selection;
    the next bounded experiment is pairwise MIS, not another surface gate;
  - mode 5 bounds energy but retains a red winner like mode 3: amplification
    and proposal correlation are two separate defects.
Mode 6 is a selected-center-only pairwise-MIS proof.  It freshly evaluates
both samples in both receiver domains and applies pair-local balance weights
before streaming.  It deliberately disables empty-center rescue and never
uses the mode-0/5 post-selection global denominator.  This separates ordinary
spatial reuse from rescue feedback and tests whether the unbounded growth is
the expected support-collapse failure of basic correction.  The construction
is derived from the normalization proof in the repo-owned clean-room GI
spatial implementation; direct-light replay uses no reconnection Jacobian.

Accepted direct-reuse checkpoint
--------------------------------

The final direct-only runtime A/B promoted these sub-policy defaults in commit
d5929c127:

    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtDirectProposalParity  1
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtDuplication           1
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtSpatialProofMode      0

Together with direct-target PDF parity, stable light fingerprints, non-recursive
rescue, unique neighbor admission, the adaptive duplication cap, and maximum
history M/age 32, mode 0 no longer ran away in the accepted scenes. Age 63 can
still amplify a few isolated regions and has no observed quality justification.
Mode 3 remains useful to bracket estimator failures, and mode 6 remains a
pairwise proof; neither replaces mode 0 by default.

The performance result was also positive. In the user's tested scene proposal
parity 1 was approximately 2 ms faster than the fallback. S0 was approximately
2.3 ms faster while about 0.4 ms moved elsewhere, and compact32 saved roughly
0.8 ms. These are scene/capture results, not universal constants, but they are
sufficient to reject reverting to the sparse fallback merely because it issues
fewer nominal trials.

Remaining direct-light limitation
---------------------------------

UPT still loses some emissive lights at reproducible player portal positions;
legacy clean DI does not. The two lanes share the upstream emissive inventory,
manager current payload/ranges, and host-built power/area CDF, so another cap or
universe restriction is not justified by current evidence. The strongest
downstream difference is legacy D0's default-on previous-best reseed before RIS.
UPT currently has only fresh D0 proposals and a rejected/default-off previous-
best experiment in T0.

The next spatial-related change is therefore not another S0 correction mode.
Port the legacy-shaped previous-best reseed into D0 with stable-ID translation
and duplicate exclusion, then instrument selection, dense mapping, exact replay,
target/source PDF, visibility, and final reservoir retention for one emitter in
a working and broken portal position. Only after that proof should indirect
path spatial shifting or reciprocal-neighbor policy work resume.
