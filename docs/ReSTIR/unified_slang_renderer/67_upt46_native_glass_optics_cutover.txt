UPT-46 - Native Slang glass optics cutover
============================================

Status
------

Required replacement. The temporarily admitted clean-RTXDI HLSL material-
feature pipelines are not the owning UPT architecture. They carry a large
legacy Vulkan RT PSO, a broad descriptor ABI, clean-DI receiver replacement,
and old feature-registry state. UPT must stop instantiating them.

Preserved verified contract
---------------------------

- compact32 is the sole P0/D0/T0/S0 receiver;
- glass never replaces that receiver;
- the pane is a current-frame optical interaction only;
- DLSS RR and UPT temporal both follow the behind-pane receiver and motion;
- optical work is bounded to one pane capture, one transmitted/refracted ray,
  one reflected/mirror ray, fixed-size sidecars, and one late compose;
- no full-frame per-frame buffer clear and no additional reusable reservoir;
- Vulkan and Slang only.

Native pass decomposition
-------------------------

UPT.G0 Pane capture

Produce one narrow pane record per pixel. Store only the fields required by
optics: world position or hit distance, octahedral geometric/shading normal,
view direction, material feature index, UV, stable material identity and a
validity/version word. Do not publish depth, motion, RR guides or a receiver.

UPT.G1 Optical transport

Consume G0, compact geometry/material services and TLAS. Compact valid pane
pixels before traversal, then resolve two independent optical path vertices:

    T lane: one straight or refracted continuation through the pane;
    R lane: one perfect/specular reflected continuation.

Each lane must produce a canonical UPT surface value through the shared UPT
geometry/material implementation, then invoke the same bounded UPT D0
light/emission evaluation used for ordinary receivers. A miss samples the
renderer sky. The lane output contains resolved radiance and its Fresnel/
transmission weight; it is not merely attenuation or a one-light legacy shade.
It cannot write the ordinary compact32 receiver, history, reservoir pages,
motion vectors, or RR guides.

This is deliberately a distinct optical estimator. It is not a second primary
renderer: G1 may not carry private geometry routing, texture decoding, alpha
testing, material reconstruction or light-selection logic. Those operations
must be shared Slang UPT services. The optical specialization only owns ray
direction, bounded traversal, lane-local receiver storage and composition
weights.

UPT.G2 Late compose

Consume UPT R0 and the three optical sidecars. Apply bounded bilinear normal-
map distortion, transmission attenuation and already-Fresnel-weighted reflected
radiance. Write the presentation texture consumed by DLSS RR or presentation.
This is a small compute pipeline; it traces no rays and selects no lights.

Vulkan ABI rules
----------------

- Each native shader gets a dedicated minimal binding layout in
  PathTraceUnifiedPtState. Do not reuse the clean-DI sentinel/material-feature
  binding layout or registry.
- Host constants live in PathTraceUnifiedPt.h and every allocation uses
  sizeof(the host type), with a static_assert for each frozen register count.
- Pipeline creation is lazy, keyed only by the native glass feature mode, and
  failures disable glass rather than terminating the renderer.
- The UPT build graph emits only Slang SPIR-V artifacts. No HLSL RT blob and no
  DX12 variant is part of UPT-46.

Ordered implementation
----------------------

1. Add G2 first, fed by the currently proven sidecars, to replace the legacy
   glass composer and validate the minimal layout/presentation boundary.
2. Add G1 and compare sidecar debug views against the temporary producer:
   transmitted/refracted resolved radiance, reflected/mirror resolved
   radiance, distortion offset/weight and both sky-miss paths.
3. Add the narrow G0 pane record and remove the second wide P0 capture.
4. Remove all UPT calls to EnsurePathTraceCleanRtxdiDiMaterialFeaturePassPipelines,
   DispatchPathTraceCleanRtxdiDiTransmissionPsrPass and
   DispatchPathTraceCleanRtxdiDiGlassComposePass.
5. Remove UPT's dependency on clean-DI feature runtime buffers, placeholder
   SRVs, wide primary history, clean-DI blue noise and the sentinel layout.

Verified implementation checkpoint - native G2, 2026-08-14
-----------------------------------------------------------

The first cutover slice is complete. `upt45_glass_compose.slang` replaces the
legacy clean-RTXDI glass composer in the UPT route. Its complete Vulkan ABI is
four texture SRVs, one RGBA16F UAV and a statically asserted 32-byte push
constant block. It traces no ray and has no bindless, material-feature,
sentinel, wide-history or clean-DI receiver binding.

UPT no longer creates or dispatches the clean-RTXDI glass shader library. The
temporary adapter explicitly admits only the transmission-producer
registration; that producer remains the next G1/G0 cutover boundary. The old
clean-DI composer stays available to the old clean-DI route and is not shared
with UPT.

The startup crash was independent but exposed by this work: sky-resolve b0 had
grown from 16 to 32 bytes while its host buffer allocation remained a literal
16. The shared host ABI now has a 32-byte static assertion and its allocation
uses `sizeof(PathTraceSkySurfaceResolveConstants)`, preventing the oversized
NVRHI `writeBuffer` operation.

Runtime correction after native G2 validation - 2026-08-14
-----------------------------------------------------------

G2 proved only the minimal compositor ABI. Runtime showed that neither useful
optical path existed behind it. The temporary optics-only producer deliberately
disabled reflection-PSR surface ownership to protect compact32. Its remaining
reflection sidecar shades exact hit emission plus at most one analytic light;
it is not a fully lit reflected UPT path. The refracted-transmission option
computes a direction and attenuation, but optics-only returns before tracing,
resolving and shading the transmitted receiver. Consequently no cvar can make
the current bridge provide real reflection or refraction.

RTXDI Full Sample confirms the missing architecture. Its final Glass pass is a
separate post-lighting ray walk: it accumulates transmissive throughput, adds a
dedicated reflected secondary, and continues exact zero-roughness opaque hits
as mirror rays. Its PT PSR buffers are a separate final-shading/temporal guide
mechanism. Therefore UPT must not conflate the ordinary behind-pane compact32
receiver with R/T optical receivers. G1 now owns two bounded lane-local
receivers and must shade them through shared UPT services before G2 composes.

First optical image correction, 2026-08-14
------------------------------------------

Runtime proved that G1's first transmitted-ray implementation violated the
receiver ownership established by UPT-45. P0 had already continued through
the pane and R0 contained the authoritative behind-pane result with D0/T0/S0,
indirect lighting, material coverage, sky and emissive transport. G1 replaced
that complete result with one freshly traced and one-shot-shaded receiver on
every pane because the inherited refraction-strength cvar defaults to 0.25.
This caused missing transmission and black or incomplete objects behind glass.

G1 now publishes alpha-1 attenuation for transmission so G2 preserves and
weights canonical R0. No transmitted RayQuery is launched until a distinct
transmitted receiver/reservoir exists. The compiler log states `R1+T0` and
`canonicalTransmission=1`; the G1 ray ceiling is consequently one reflection
continuation plus one selected-light visibility ray.

Runtime also proved the reflected one-shot lane was about four times noisier
than legacy. Its RIS schedule incorrectly used the inverse-square secondary
bounce budget even though G1 is a primary optical lane. It now uses the full
accepted primary UPT light schedule. This is a bounded candidate-budget
correction only: it does not add candidate visibility rays or admit zero-target
samples. If non-brush hits remain black after this A/B, the remaining cause is
reflection receiver/reuse ownership rather than the shared geometry/material
decoder, which is identical to the accepted UPT indirect-hit decoder.

RR reflection guides and sky transmission, 2026-08-14
------------------------------------------------------

Runtime accepted the corrected reflection image but DLSS Ray Reconstruction
removed most of it. The beauty input already contains reflected radiance; the
missing classification was the legacy RR specular contract. G1 now blends a
bounded 0.35 share of the reflected endpoint's material F0 into the existing
behind-pane specular-albedo guide and writes the actual reflection hit distance.
Sky reflection uses normalized chromaticity, not HDR radiance, in the albedo-
range guide. Reflected radiance remains exclusively in RR input color.

The transmission failure had two opposite endpoint cases. Compact32 correctly
represents an ordinary surface behind glass, so replacing it with a one-shot
transmitted shade destroys indirect and reuse. Compact32 cannot represent a
sky/environment miss, so preserving R0 alone produces black sky. G1 therefore

    valid compact32 surface -> preserve R0 and apply attenuation;
    invalid compact32       -> trace straight through supported panes;

    transmitted triangle    -> fail back to canonical attenuation ownership.

R1+T0; only pixels whose glass continuation has no representable canonical
surface launch Tmiss. The compiler log states `R1+Tmiss`,

Native material/transport parity correction, 2026-08-14
--------------------------------------------------------

Runtime disproved two assumptions in the first native G1 cut. First, the
legacy transmission sidecar is not an authoritative UPT producer. Reading it
for thin-sheet weights allowed a cleared or stale legacy payload to black out
the complete behind-pane UPT result. G1 now derives transmittance, thickness,
IOR, strength, reflection boost and transmission floor directly from the
glass material-feature parameter record. Slot t36 was removed from the native
descriptor ABI.

Second, hardware opacity is a CPU acceleration decision while glass is a
shader material feature. The accepted legacy optical service forces
non-opaque traversal so its any-hit classifier cannot be bypassed by a stale
or incomplete opaque-geometry classification. Native G1 RayQuery now does the
same. Every candidate is canonicalized, glass candidates are skipped, alpha
coverage is evaluated, and the first ordinary surface is committed. This is
required both for transmission through stacked panes and for mirrors which
see a window.

The RR guide now describes the camera-visible interface Fresnel/reflection
response. The former endpoint-based guide made bright sky/emissive endpoints
look maximally specular and dark reflected endpoints nearly diffuse, coupling
RR retention to background luminance. Reflected radiance remains in the RR
beauty input; only bounded interface response enters specular albedo, with the
actual reflected hit distance unchanged.

Finally, the shared Slang secondary-material evaluator now applies the same
packed material classifier, full-metal override and zero-roughness override as
primary shading. These were missing from reflected and indirect secondary
hits, so valid rigid/skinned geometry could be decoded but shaded using the
wrong BSDF or collapse to black. The correction uses existing 112-byte
material fields and adds no lookup, persistent buffer or ray.

Resolved Tmiss radiance is no longer overwritten by the cosmetic source-color
distortion sample in G2. Ordinary alpha-1 transmission retains the existing
distortion behavior; alpha-2 sky/environment endpoints retain their resolved
radiance.

Runtime optical-endpoint and RR-guide correction, 2026-08-14

Runtime proved that treating only a hardware RayQuery miss as an environment
endpoint was insufficient. Doom presents its sky through committed sky-shell
triangles. Compact32 cannot encode either that terminal or a terminal emissive,
so an invalid behind-pane compact receiver now launches the already-bounded
transmission continuation and classifies the committed material: sky-shells
resolve from the environment cube, emissives resolve through the shared UPT
material/shading service, and ordinary triangles continue to fail back to
canonical R0 ownership. No additional ray, buffer, retry or clear was added.

The RR specular export contract is deliberately a reconstruction guide in this
renderer, not physical F0. G1 now duplicates the exact final HDR reflection
contribution into the RR specular channel. For glass this is
`reflectedRadiance * reflectionWeight * composeBoost`; for an exact mirror it
is the same Fresnel-weighted radiance written to beauty. The export is not
saturated, and the pre-existing behind-surface specular contribution is
preserved. This makes RR see the same reflection brightness as the composed
color rather than a small interface-Fresnel hint.

The native UPT route now always consumes its bounded distortion sidecar. The
legacy clean-DI distortion cvar defaults off and was silently disabling G2
despite the UPT front-pane producer publishing valid offsets. Resolved

room lighting while present in the view TLAS, then admitted the light as soon
as it left the view, proves that the ordinary UPT traversal families were
committing glass as opaque. Fixing only G1 could never repair that behavior.

The accepted transparent-interface decision is now shared by every production
UPT RayQuery family:

- D0 continuation and direct/indirect visibility in UPT04;
- T0 direct visibility and indirect replay in UPT07;
- S0 direct visibility in UPT09; and
- dedicated optical continuation in UPT46.

Each candidate first resolves its authoritative material identity. Proven
portal-window or object-glass materials are skipped; missing or invalid
identity remains solid. The change adds no ray, candidate, retry, persistent
buffer or clear. It restores the required invariant that moving a pane into or
out of the view-owned TLAS cannot toggle light transport through that pane.

sparse optical subset, while the noisy beauty image also contains reflected
transport selected by the ordinary combined reservoir. R0 now writes the exact
unsaturated HDR resolved color to the RR specular guide whenever the selected

the exact Fresnel/compose-weighted reflection contribution written to beauty;

diffuse/specular mixtures retain the primary material guide because their

Resolved sky/emissive transmission now participates in distortion as well.
G2 bilinearly samples the resolved transmission image for alpha-2 endpoints,

Legacy-shaped primary pane capture correction, 2026-08-14
----------------------------------------------------------

The subtype-complete traversal change did not address the actual split at the
camera interface. The accepted legacy renderer classifies a transmitting pane
from the union of its validated material-feature record, packed translucent
subtype and portal/object-glass fallback flags. The UPT lean P0 any-hit path
had diverged: its deterministic mode-1 pass-through and mode-2 optical capture
accepted only the material-feature verdict. If a feature record was valid but
did not describe glass, mode 1 could still reach the receiver through the later
stochastic fallback while mode 2 failed to capture the pane. That produces the
observed combination of a correct stainwall receiver behind
`textures/glass/glass1` and no native transmission for the pane itself.

UPT lean P0 now uses the legacy-shaped union before the stochastic coverage
path. It recognizes only a valid transmission feature, translucent subtype 4,
translucent subtype 1 with the object-glass bit, or the accepted portal/object
fallback bits. Mode 1 deterministically ignores that pane and records the
crossing; mode 2 deterministically accepts the first pane for optical capture.
No material-name heuristic and no broad translucent admission were added.

G1 now also reconstructs the authoritative packed class from the primary
record's stored instance/primitive identity. This covers compact primary
records whose `materialAndSurface.w` contains only the broad surface class.
The lookup uses the existing metadata-only geometry resolver and does not fetch
vertices, add rays or allocate/clear a buffer.

Legacy transparent-modifier continuation correction, 2026-08-14

failing panes: working panes disappeared from mirror rays, while failing panes
terminated the ray and displayed their opaque grime texture. This proves the
remaining split is in G1 continuation traversal, not P0 pane capture or final

The accepted legacy transmission predicate does not recognize glass alone. It
also treats filter decals, additive decals, white-key additive decals and the

multi-stage Doom 3 glass declarations whose grime or environment modifier can

G1 optical continuation now uses the same exact legacy flag set. Classification
stays on UPT's narrow routed material/class resolvers so the candidate loop does
not import full vertex and attribute decoding. The broader rule is local to the
bounded G1 R/T continuation and does not change D0/T0/S0 visibility,
alpha-clipped geometry or ordinary decal composition. Missing geometry or

Crosshair transmission decision diagnostic, 2026-08-14
--------------------------------------------------------

Reflection endpoint-shading parity correction, 2026-08-14
----------------------------------------------------------

Runtime accepted the shared visibility correction, then exposed two independent
reflection-lane gaps: analytic illumination was weak/missing, and some
transparent emissive cards disappeared from reflections.

The accepted legacy secondary reflection shade is exact endpoint emission plus
analytic-only RIS using `r_pathTracingReflectionSecondarySamples` (default 8,
clamped 1--16), selecting at most one analytic shade and one visibility ray.
It does not mix emissive NEE candidates into that small analytic budget. The
former UPT46 implementation reused the combined D0 light schedule, allowing
emissive proposals to starve analytic selection, and normalized by all trials
including rejected zero-target candidates.

UPT46 now uses the same bounded shape over UPT's current analytic-light
universe. It performs analytic-only RIS with the existing candidate-count and
shadow CVars, normalizes by positive candidates, and still commits at most one
shadow ray. Exact opaque-hit emission continues to use the UPT OpenPBR material
provider. Optical traversal additionally evaluates the nearest skipped
transparent emissive card and adds its textured endpoint emission when it lies
in front of the committed hit, restoring the legacy pass-through-emissive
contract without introducing a second reflection ray.

executable `6115828209B806CDD34715E485721FBBE79D56F8BB6653B536BEBC85EA6775CB`,

natural `84021F5C3587639FBBF464E7B2B67C43DE4FB7934F214DF415A56DF2014473BE`.

Temporal ownership remains explicit. Stage-6 straight-through transmission

Deterministic flat glass-reflection correction, 2026-08-14
-----------------------------------------------------------

The reflection endpoint-shading parity trial above is superseded. Runtime
testing showed that importing legacy analytic RIS into G1 recreated the wrong
class of reflection: stochastic direct lighting and transparent-emissive
endpoint discovery made a clear-glass reflection noisy, while still falling
short of the separately denoised zero-roughness opaque-reflection lane.

Local reference audit confirms that RTXDI FullSample glass uses a different
contract from its ReSTIR/PT mirror path. The glass pass traces one sharp
secondary ray, skips transmissive geometry, performs a small deterministic
material lookup at the terminal and composites that signal with Fresnel. The
full ReSTIR path instead owns opaque delta surfaces and can publish reflected
depth, normal and motion to its denoiser. Those are related features but are
not one estimator.

UPT46 G1 now follows the cheap glass contract:

* one deterministic reflected closest-hit query;
* supported glass and transparent pane modifiers remain non-occluding;
* a miss returns the resolved sky;
* a triangle returns the decoded flat material signal
  `max(baseColor, specularF0)`, retaining metals without double-adding lobes;
* no light-universe selection, NEE candidates, shadow ray, endpoint emission,
  GI evaluation or reflection recursion;
* the exact HDR contribution written to beauty is also written to the DLSS-RR
  specular guide, with the reflected hit distance.

Canonical transmission is unchanged. Its narrow sky/emissive fallback remains
only for endpoints that compact32 cannot represent, while normal stage-6
transmission continues to attenuate the already T0/S0-reused R0 result.

The discarded RIS host ABI was removed: G1 no longer consumes
`r_pathTracingReflectionSecondarySamples` or
`r_pathTracingReflectionSecondaryShadows`, and their candidate/shadow bits are
no longer packed into the optical control record. Unused light-selection,
proposal and replay imports were removed from UPT46. Both natural-light and
compact64-light Vulkan variants consequently compile to the same 198448-byte
SPIR-V blob, SHA-256
`7D3E738C4601372FDC5063C4C0B69270247F5FB02E6F137A7ACE05F9506D2678`.
The targeted UPT46 build and full Vulkan release build pass. No DX12 shader was
compiled. The final deployed executable is 16435200 bytes, SHA-256
`2EA442689E6DA34A5320BF03D22F99F9AC1D61812DAD40B1DF1D68B14CCB1BD1`;
the deployed natural and compact64 blobs match the source artifacts exactly.

Runtime acceptance is deliberately narrow: reflections must be stable in a
still view, textured rigid/skinned/static terminals and metal terminals must be
recognizable, emissive or analytic-light selection must not boil, glass inside
the reflected view must remain ignored, and DLSS-RR must preserve the same
reflection signal during motion. Fully denoised opaque zero-roughness
reflections remain a later reflected-receiver PSR task.

Flat-response and distortion-guide correction, 2026-08-14
-----------------------------------------------------------

Runtime accepted the deterministic one-ray architecture but rejected two
output details. Returning unit-intensity decoded albedo made dark scene
surfaces glow much brighter in glass than at the source, and UPT46 published
only the reflected radiance to RR's specular channel. UPT45 distorted beauty
color while leaving every RR geometry guide at the original screen pixel.

The deterministic terminal now matches RTXDI FullSample's scale: decoded
`max(baseColor, specularF0)` is multiplied by fixed ambient
`(0.05, 0.045, 0.03)`. This remains noise-free and light-universe independent.
The hit's diffuse albedo is blended into RR albedo at the accepted legacy 0.35
material ratio; reflected F0 is blended into RR specular, and the exact HDR
beauty contribution remains present in specular so reconstruction cannot erase
the reflection.

Distortion now preserves one coherent RR surface tuple. Before G2, the six
source guides are copied into dedicated no-clear outputs. Only distorted glass
pixels are overwritten by bilinear samples at the exact beauty offset:
albedo, specular albedo, normalized normal/roughness, linear depth, world
position and motion vectors. Source and destination are distinct, so adjacent
glass pixels cannot recursively consume already-distorted guides. DLSS-RR is
fed the resulting guide set; specular hit distance remains the actual reflected
ray distance.

The additional guide allocation is persistent per resolution and has no
per-frame full-buffer clear. G2 performs six bounded texture copies, then its
existing full-resolution compose dispatch. Targeted UPT45/UPT46 builds and the
full Vulkan release build pass. No DX12 shader was compiled. Deployed hashes:
UPT45 `6295A86B14B4D8B165D0CFD23D57930D1D7A536D9E45C07AC15FD29BE3EEF5A3`,
UPT46 natural/compact64
`3D6248D31F259C69BF9BABAEA2E0BD3118CF51A97D0DE6EF85B8D2A03137018B`,
executable `04F4E51B2CEA26EE049B35B714A370C3E676A9B9648C7FFB2FB613CB1C2532FC`.

Runtime acceptance must check brightness parity against a directly visible dark
receiver, RR albedo/specular debug views, and motion across a strongly distorted
normal-mapped pane. Normal edges, depth discontinuities and motion must follow
the same offset as beauty rather than remaining attached to the undistorted
background.
