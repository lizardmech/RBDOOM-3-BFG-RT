Renderer purpose and present boundary
-------------------------------------

UPT is a Vulkan-only Slang renderer built beside the legacy clean DI/GI lanes.
It is not a refactor of those lanes. Its long-term target is the ReSTIR PT
Enhanced path-tree design: direct light is a short selected path and indirect
light is a longer selected path in one reservoir stream. Materials remain
separate providers rather than being embedded in ReSTIR passes.

The direct-only route is now useful and fast enough to move beyond D0/T0/S0
micro-tuning. The next large unknown is the unified D0 path with indirect
continuation/secondary NEE enabled. Do not begin that extension until the
remaining UPT-only emissive acquisition loss is understood or fixed; otherwise
the indirect comparison will be contaminated by a known direct-light defect.

Accepted low-level and ABI fixes - do not regress
-------------------------------------------------

1. Slang RayQuery Vulkan bindings

   Initial device removals were descriptor-layout errors, not a RayQuery or
   Slang hardware failure. The Vulkan/NVRHI binding-offset contract, TLAS slot,
   push constants, and reflected descriptor order must remain exact. Later
   duplicate t5 and UPT-08 SRV ordering crashes were also binding-layout bugs.
   Treat reflection and the live binding set as one ABI.

2. Two fully written 64-byte pages, no per-frame clear

   D0/T0/S0 fully cover their destination. Canonical empty/count-only encoding
   makes stale bytes self-invalid. Pages are cleared only when allocated; reset
   and camera/scene invalidation advance host metadata. Do not add a 225 MiB
   per-frame clear. Future sparse sidecars require stamps or compact active
   storage rather than relying on page metadata.

3. Frame serial and page-role advancement

   History metadata includes a host frame serial. History is usable only when
   its serial is exactly current-1. A skipped T0/S0 frame must advance or repair
   page ownership consistently; otherwise an N-2 reservoir page is compared
   against N-1 primary surfaces and portal crossings look like a persistent
   flush. This fixed the portal temporal desynchronization. Preserve the source
   comment documenting this invariant.

4. Stable direct-light identity

   Reservoir word2 is the current dense manager index+1. Word3 is a fingerprint
   of (type, identityA, identityB, materialOrLightId). sourceIndex is a
   frame-local source-array address and must never be treated as stable light
   identity. T0 maps through the manager previous-to-current table, then checks
   the fingerprint. This fixed animated-light ID churn/strobing.

5. Doom-scale visibility rays

   Primary-direct visibility uses both geometric- and shading-normal horizon
   tests, a 0.75-unit signed geometric-normal offset, 0.25 units along the ray,
   and a 0.5-unit endpoint margin. RayQuery uses non-opaque candidate control
   and ignores the selected emissive instance/primitive tuple. Reverting to an
   unoffset origin, 0.02 endpoint margin, FORCE_OPAQUE, or no self-ignore causes
   self-intersections on real Doom maps.

6. Temporal visibility ownership

   R0 is intentionally trace-free. Therefore T0 performs at most one
   winner-only visibility RayQuery for a reused winner whose current-frame
   visibility is not already proven. On failure it restores the untouched D0
   reservoir; rejected history mass is not retained. Do not remove this ray on
   the assumption that RTXDI's resampling helper is trace-free: its surrounding
   pipeline re-establishes visibility elsewhere.

7. Temporal surface search discipline

   Search mode 2 dithers reprojection and probes up to nine radius-4 surface
   positions. The first compatible surface wins, and only then is its reservoir
   loaded. Reservoir content must never steer the surface search. The rejected
   fixed/favorable-neighbor variants produced watery noise, trails, and hidden
   spatial reuse inside T0.

8. Compact receiver and authored smoothing

   CompactReceiver=2 uses a 32-byte hot D0 receiver plus a separate 32-byte cold
   history sidecar for T0/S0. The lean primary route also preserves authored
   vertex normals/smoothing. Do not replace authored smoothing with a fixed
   angle or recomputed face normal.

9. Direct target measure and proposal parity

   DirectTargetPdfParity=1 stores the RTXDI-shaped target
   luminance(BRDF*Li*cos)/solidAnglePdf, with technique MIS applied once in the
   scalar RIS weight. DirectProposalParity=1 uses one CDF-selected emissive
   trial plus up to 32 range-stratified analytic trials. Changing only one pass
   to another target convention invalidates D0/T0/S0 reuse math.

10. Duplication control and bounded age

   U0/U1 publish/count a stable sample ID over a 17x17 neighborhood and reduce
   the next-frame temporal M cap in correlated regions. MaxHistoryM=32 and
   MaxAge=32 passed the accepted mode-0 test. Age 63 remains a stress/no-expiry
   setting and can still amplify isolated regions. Spatial mode 3 is a useful
   bounded 1/M diagnostic, not the production default.

11. Non-recursive empty-center rescue

   An empty-center rescue winner is consumable for the current frame but is
   stamped so it cannot donate recursively through later temporal/spatial
   frames. Neighbor offsets are unique and edge-clamped pixel duplicates are
   rejected. Do not restore with-replacement recursive donation.

12. Monolithic D0 remains the accepted performance shape

   The combined direct/indirect and continuation split experiments serialized
   work and duplicated traversal. They were slower than the approximately
   0.9 ms monolithic D0 in the accepted direct scene. Do not reopen a split
   solely because its individual marker is smaller; compare the complete sum
   and RT-core work.

Performance observations worth preserving
------------------------------------------

The small test scene reached the 120 FPS cap at 2560x1440 with textured models.
CompactReceiver=2 saved roughly 0.8 ms in the user's capture. In the tested
larger scene DirectProposalParity=1 was unexpectedly about 2 ms faster than the
fallback; S0 itself was about 2.3 ms faster while moving about 0.4 ms elsewhere.
The compact route also reduced render-thread pressure and improved FPS in the
CPU-bound parts of the game. Treat in-game FPS as secondary at high frame rates;
use Nsight marker sums for small differences.

The early performance investigation also found discrete slow/fast GPU states
whose delay moved between substantial dispatches. It was not tied to one shader
and Nsight's clock lock did not explain the within-capture change. That issue is
recorded separately in docs/optimize and is not an excuse to discard measured
UPT payload/bandwidth wins.

Emissive state: what is fixed
-----------------------------

The following are distinct contracts and have been repaired:

    primary visible self-emission is a local R0 term and never enters reuse;
    back-facing Doom panels follow the explicit two-sided emissive policy;
    D0/T0/S0 replay exact triangle UV/material emission rather than a flat
        manager radiance estimate;
    conditional door/signage stages use the lean nearest-signage tuple;
    static-bucket replay uses the TLAS instance/primitive identity rather than
        confusing it with the canonical surface record;
    stale/dynamic material-state ownership that created ghost emitters was
        corrected in the current worktree.

Bucket routing explained an emission-color/white-replay defect, but disabling
bucket routes did not fix emitters disappearing. Do not conflate those issues.

Current blocker: UPT-only emissive acquisition loss
---------------------------------------------------

Some emitters disappear reproducibly in particular player portal positions,
often inside or one portal away. Running through other portals can make them
return; one standing position can reliably turn them off. Legacy clean DI is
not affected. Analytic portal-domain, static-bucket route, and other tested
route controls did not change the disappearance. The problem is not the global
emissive cap: observed inventories are well below the 4096-triangle cap.

The important comparison is:

    shared upstream data
        Both lanes consume the same emissive triangle inventory, the light
        manager's current payload/ranges, and the same host-built power/area CDF.

    legacy D0
        Before ordinary RIS, it reprojects the prior best reservoir, translates
        the light through stable previous-to-current identity, replays it at the
        current receiver, and streams it once as a current proposal. The default
        r_pathTracingCleanRtxdiDiBestLights=1 enables this D0 reseed.

    UPT D0
        It currently relies on fresh CDF selection. Its previous-best experiment
        lives in T0, defaults off, and was visually broken (dark/overexposed).

This makes the leading next fix a legacy-parity D0 previous-best reseed with
stable-ID translation and duplicate exclusion. Do not simply enable
r_pathTracingUnifiedPtTemporalPreviousBest; that is a different stage and its
estimator was not accepted.

If that does not close the portal-position loss, instrument the stages in this
order, using one selected emitter identity across both positions:

    1. CDF interval selected and dense manager index resolved;
    2. dense index maps to the expected emissive replay tuple;
    3. tuple/triangle/UV/material replay succeeds;
    4. exact endpoint emission is finite and colored;
    5. target and source PDF are positive;
    6. visibility ray reaches the receiver without self-hit;
    7. reservoir selection/finalization retains the candidate.

Do not add candidate-count guesses, portal-local caps, or another emissive
universe until that boundary records where the same light is lost.

Implemented D0 acquisition checkpoint, 2026-08-08
-------------------------------------------------

The reported uniform-mixture experiment led to a verified pre-proposal defect.
UPT used the emissive CDF structured buffer's physical GetDimensions result as
the searchable entry count. The allocation is intentionally capacity-reused,
so a smaller portal-frame publication left a stale high-water tail visible to
UPT's binary search. The scene already publishes the exact current count and
valid bit, which legacy clean DI consumes through its current-frame constants.

The implemented repair uses that exact current count/validity and treats the
physical dimension only as an OOB guard. Invalid current weighted publication
uses the existing typed uniform fallback. It does not add a PDF floor, enlarge
the portal domain, shrink/reallocate the buffer, or add random candidates.

D0 now also has an emissive-only legacy-shaped previous-best reseed behind
r_pathTracingUnifiedPtD0PreviousBest=1. It validates prior receiver history,
stable remap, current identity, exact stored barycentric replay, current
material/target, duplicate exclusion, and winner visibility. Dynamic-history
emissives remain rejected. Analytic previous-best samples remain excluded, so
fresh analytic behavior is unchanged. The merge preserves UPT's target-scaled
mean-mass convention and uses no ray beyond the existing selected D0 visibility
query. T0 was not used as compensation.

Diagnostics now distinguish:

    previous lookup attempted/succeeded;
    remap, replay, current-target, and visibility failure;
    duplicate rejection and successful previous admission;
    fresh analytic versus fresh emissive selection;
    unavailable weighted CDF plus current count/capacity/valid/zero-PDF skips.

Next implementation sequence
----------------------------

1. D0 emissive acquisition is complete and runtime-accepted. Preserve the
   exact current CDF count contract, previous-best rejection rules, one-ray
   ceiling, and stage diagnostics as regression coverage.

2. In a separately authorized lane, enable the existing unified family and
   add/repair the first indirect continuation and secondary NEE path-tree work.
   Preserve one shared selected path; do not create separate DI and GI
   reservoirs or tag-specific Lambert kernels.

3. Validate the unified image before adding more reuse policy. Compare direct-
   only versus unified D0/R0 with T0/S0 disabled first, then admit temporal and
   spatial shift providers for indirect paths explicitly. A partial provider
   must reject an unsupported history event without changing current M/age.

4. Only after the one-continuation unified path is correct, extend toward the
   user-validated three-bounce/Russian-roulette target in UPT-10A. No current
   ABI choice should prevent it, but no extra queue/buffer/dispatch is admitted
   without measured justification.

    base/renderprogs2/spirv/

Do not compile or deploy DX12 shaders. Slang UPT targets are declared in
neo/shaders/CMakeLists.txt and emit under their slang_uptXX directories.

Gotcha: editing an HLSLI or RT HLSL include may not retrigger DXC. Delete the
affected .rt.bin before the build and verify the rebuilt blob's timestamp is
newer than the source edit. Always verify deployed EXE/blob size and mtime when
a runtime A/B shows no change.
