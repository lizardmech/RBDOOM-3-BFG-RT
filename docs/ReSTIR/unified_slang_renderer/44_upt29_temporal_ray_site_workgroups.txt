UPT-29: temporal ray-site and workgroup attribution
===================================================

Trigger
-------

Corrected production-shaped captures still changed materially with small
camera movement even after alpha-clipped geometry was hidden. The aggregate
probe could prove that the slow view performed more programmable traversal,
but it could not identify which temporal ray owned that work or whether the
cost was broad or caused by a few divergent lanes per workgroup.

The latest comparable probe-12 pair measured:

    slow median                 13.486 ms
    fast median                 12.845 ms
    slow/fast Proceed mean      0.122 / 0.102 per pixel
    slow/fast geometry mean     0.304 / 0.270 per pixel
    slow/fast material mean     0.301 / 0.266 per pixel
    slow/fast light-load mean   3.639 / 3.727 per pixel

The slow view therefore has about 20 percent more Proceed work and 13 percent
more geometry/material work while performing fewer light loads. Remaining
programmable candidates are glass/translucent surfaces or mixed non-opaque
BLAS chunks, not the hidden alpha-clipped surfaces. The pair is a 0.641 ms
difference, not the earlier reported 20--30 percent discontinuity, so the next
capture must attribute work rather than infer from camera content.

Implementation
--------------

Probe 12 now attributes four metrics to four bounded ray sites:

    site 0 history indirect replay
    site 1 reciprocal indirect replay
    site 2 final indirect visibility
    site 3 final direct visibility

Each site is one packed dword with saturated 8-bit ray, Proceed, geometry and
material counts. Aggregate 32-bit counters remain unchanged. The violation
word moves from 8 to 12, producing a 13-dword record per pixel.

The GPU path uses thread-owned stores only. It adds no atomics, full-buffer
clear or estimator work. Site attribution begins immediately around the four
existing ray-owning regions, so helper work such as an early reconnect ray is
charged to the replay site that requested it.

The delayed CPU readback reconstructs the native 8x8 dispatch groups. For each
site it prints total and active-pixel metrics, ray/Proceed-active group counts,
the number of groups with at least 1/2/4/8/16/32 Proceed-active lanes, mean
active lanes per infected group, and maximum active lanes/Proceed iterations.
Packed-byte saturation is printed and invalidates quantitative interpretation
if nonzero.

Acceptance protocol
-------------------

Use the UPT-28 production gates and capture probe 12 at the slow and fast poses.
Do not compare the absolute probe-12 time with probe 0 because the site stores
are intentionally additional observer work. Compare the four site distributions
and group infection between poses.

Interpretation is decisive:

* historyReplay growth identifies the shifted history path;
* reciprocalReplay growth identifies current-at-history cross evaluation;
* finalIndirectVisibility growth identifies shifted indirect winner proof;
* finalDirectVisibility growth identifies the direct winner proof;
* similar totals but more low-lane infected groups identifies traversal
  divergence rather than additional logical work;
* saturation requires a wider diagnostic ABI before drawing conclusions.

No renderer correction should be attempted until this capture names the site.

-----------------------
