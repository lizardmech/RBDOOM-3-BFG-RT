UPT-05 Bandwidth Bottleneck Audit
=================================

Date
----

2026-08-02

Trigger
-------

The NVIDIA FullSample trace was reported at more than 90 percent RT-core
utilization with low VRAM activity.  The corresponding rbdoom UPT trace was
reported near the VRAM-write ceiling while using only about 18 percent of the
RT cores.  That counter split is incompatible with treating the accepted
P0/D0/R0 duration alone as proof of an efficient low-level design.

Disposition
-----------

The counter observation is explained by the live shader ABI and compiled
SPIR-V.  The current no-reuse UPT path is memory-traffic heavy before temporal
or spatial reuse exists.  Temporal and spatial work remains blocked.

This audit corrects one earlier suspicion.  The live UPT P0 route uses
pathtrace_primary_surface_producer.rt.bin, not the old pathtrace_smoke.rt.bin
store helper.  Its compiled SPIR-V contains one full primary-record store and
no primary-record reload.  The old smoke shader's store/load/store sequence is
therefore not charged to the captured UPT.P0 marker.

Live contracts
--------------

At 2560 x 1440 there are 3,686,400 receiver pixels.

Primary receiver
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The inherited PathTracePrimarySurfaceRecord is 176 bytes:

    11 x 16-byte fields
    one page                    648,806,400 bytes     618.750 MiB
    current + previous pages 1,297,612,800 bytes   1,237.500 MiB

The record mixes current shading state with cold or later-use state:

    version/valid/debug/class flags
    world position and view depth
    geometric normal and roughness
    shading normal and opacity
    view direction
    albedo and alpha cutoff
    specular F0
    emissive and height placeholder
    previous position or motion
    material/surface identities
    instance/primitive/object identities

The current UPT initial sampler directly consumes only validity plus current
position, normals, view direction, albedo, roughness, and F0.  Nevertheless a
StructuredBuffer load materializes the full 176-byte struct.

Unified reservoir
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Upt03PackedReservoir is 64 bytes:

    16 x uint32 words
    one page                    235,929,600 bytes     225.000 MiB

The 64-byte record remains subject to a separate hot/cold review before reuse.
It is not yet declared defective merely because it is larger than NVIDIA's
private layout: replay, invalid-sample, candidate-family, MIS, and future
three-bounce/RR requirements must remain representable.

P0: shared primary producer
---------------------------

The live source unconditionally performs, after the camera ray:

    StorePrimarySurfaceRecord
    StoreRayReconstructionGuides
    StoreRayReconstructionMotionGuides

For UPT-only execution, the first store is required.  UPT D0 does not bind or
consume any of the RR guide images, but P0 still writes:

    primary receiver                    176 B/pixel
    RR albedo RGBA16F                     8 B/pixel
    RR specular albedo RGBA16F            8 B/pixel
    RR normal/roughness RGBA16F            8 B/pixel
    RR depth R32F                         4 B/pixel
    RR hit distance R32F                  4 B/pixel
    RR position RGBA32F                  16 B/pixel
    RR reset mask R32UI                   4 B/pixel
    minimum UPT-only output              228 B/pixel

This is 840,499,200 bytes or 801.563 MiB of output per 1440p dispatch.  If
motion-vector export is enabled, RGBA16F motion, RG16F RR motion, and R32UI
motion-mask stores add up to another 16 B/pixel.

At the representative 2.4 ms P0 duration, the minimum output stores alone
correspond to about 350 GB/s.  This excludes acceleration-structure traffic,
geometry/material/texture reads, and ray payload work.  Low RT-core occupancy
beside high memory activity is therefore expected from the present contract.

Compiled-SPIR-V proof
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The deployed Vulkan blob is:

    base/renderprogs2/spirv/builtin/pathtracing/
        pathtrace_primary_surface_producer.rt.bin

spirv-dis reports:

    one OpStore to PrimarySurfaceHistoryCurrent
    zero OpLoad of PathTracePrimarySurfaceRecord
    30 static OpImageWrite instructions across the shader's guide branches

The one-store result is why the old smoke shader's store/load/store sequence
must not be used in the UPT traffic total.

D0: unified initial sampler
---------------------------

The source calls Upt04LoadInitialSurface independently through:

    Upt04PrepareDirectPathTreeWork
    Upt04PrepareIndirectPathTreeWork

The deployed Slang SPIR-V preserves both full struct loads:

    OpDecorate primary runtime array ArrayStride 176
    first  OpLoad Upt04PrimarySurfaceRecord
    second OpLoad Upt04PrimarySurfaceRecord

Minimum logical structured traffic is therefore:

    two primary receiver loads           352 B/pixel
    one packed reservoir store             64 B/pixel
    minimum D0 structured traffic         416 B/pixel

At 1440p this is 1,533,542,400 bytes or 1,462.500 MiB per dispatch.  Dividing
that logical amount by a 1.3 ms marker produces about 1.18 TB/s, so cache reuse
must service part of it; it still demonstrates that memory-system pressure is
built into D0.  Ray-query traversal, light/material tables, geometry replay,
and texture reads are additional traffic.

R0: trace-free resolve
----------------------

The deployed Slang SPIR-V performs one full 64-byte reservoir load and one
RGBA16F output write:

    reservoir load                         64 B/pixel
    output store                             8 B/pixel
    minimum R0 traffic                      72 B/pixel

At 1440p this is 265,420,800 bytes or 253.125 MiB.  This pass is necessarily
bandwidth-oriented, but a later hot/cold reservoir split may reduce its read.

No-reuse frame total
--------------------

The minimum logical P0/D0/R0 traffic visible from these contracts is:

    P0                                     228 B/pixel
    D0                                     416 B/pixel
    R0                                      72 B/pixel
    total                                  716 B/pixel
    total at 1440p               2,639,462,400 bytes
    total at 1440p                   2,517.188 MiB/frame
    logical rate at 120 FPS                 316.7 GB/s

This total is deliberately conservative.  It excludes AS, geometry, material,
texture, light, descriptor, cache-line amplification, and copy/publication
traffic.  It is not a claimed DRAM-byte counter.  It is sufficient to show why
the shader can saturate memory paths while leaving RT cores underfed.

Immediate mechanical corrections
--------------------------------

Two corrections do not require designing the final compact temporal ABI:

1. UPT-only P0 must not export DLSS-RR guides.

   Preserve all current guide outputs whenever the clean DI/RR route requests
   the shared producer.  When UPT is the only consumer, store only its receiver
   contract.  This removes 52 B/pixel or 182.813 MiB/frame.

2. Unified D0 must load the primary receiver once.

   Pass one loaded receiver through direct-primary NEE and primary
   continuation preparation.  Direct-only and indirect-only entry points may
   retain their single-load wrappers.  This removes 176 B/pixel or
   618.750 MiB/frame.

Together these changes reduce the conservative no-reuse total from 716 to
488 B/pixel:

    before                         2,517.188 MiB/frame
    after                          1,715.625 MiB/frame
    removed                          801.563 MiB/frame
    reduction                              31.84 percent

They require a separate Nsight A/B.  An FPS-only result is not sufficient;
record P0, D0, R0, memory-write activity, RT-core utilization, and total submit.

Mechanical implementation checkpoint
------------------------------------

Both corrections were implemented and Vulkan-built on 2026-08-02.

UPT-only guide suppression
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The shared primary-producer host publishes MotionVectorInfo.y only when the
primary schedule has a live clean-DI consumer.  The producer performs its
primary receiver store unconditionally, then branches around both RR guide
export functions when that bit is zero.  UPT plus clean DI preserves the old
guide path; UPT alone suppresses it.

The rebuilt deployed P0 SPIR-V contains:

    one primary receiver OpStore
    zero primary receiver OpLoad
    one uniform load of MotionVectorInfo.y immediately after the store
    one conditional branch around the guide-export control-flow region

Single-load unified D0
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The first attempt to bind the StructuredBuffer element directly to a const
local was rejected after disassembly.  Slang scalarized that form into one base
pointer but 36 repeated 16-byte field loads, or 576 emitted logical bytes.  A
source-level shared variable was therefore not accepted as proof of shared GPU
work.

The accepted form calls Upt04LoadInitialSurface once at the composition root
and passes the resulting value to direct and indirect preparation helpers.
Both rebuilt production backends contain exactly:

    ArrayStride 176
    one primary-buffer OpAccessChain
    one OpLoad Upt04PrimarySurfaceRecord

This is verified independently in:

    upt04_initial_rayquery.bin
    upt04_initial_raygen.bin

Architectural correction required before reuse
----------------------------------------------

The 176-byte legacy record is not accepted as the UPT temporal/spatial hot
receiver ABI.  Simply retaining it and adding neighbor/history reads would
multiply the identified traffic across every reuse tap.

The next design task must define a UPT-owned compact receiver contract or an
explicit hot/cold split.  It must be derived from exact consumers:

    initial direct and continuation material evaluation
    temporal reprojection and receiver similarity
    spatial receiver similarity
    selected-sample replay and visibility
    material/surface/instance identity
    future three-bounce and Russian-roulette replay
    optional RR guide export at a later explicit boundary

Current-only shading data must not drag previous-position, diagnostics, or
cold identities through every initial/reuse load.  Conversely, compacting may
not discard the identities needed to reject invalid history or replay a
selected sample.  Candidate validity and empty-center neighbor recovery remain
foundational contracts, not after-the-fact fixes.

Revised gate
------------

The timing stability recorded in 17 remains valid, as does the absence of
steady-frame pipeline creation.  Its broad efficiency interpretation is now
qualified: acceptable elapsed time at the user's 120 FPS cap did not reveal
that the implementation was spending most of its work budget moving oversized
records and unused guide outputs.

Do not authorize temporal or spatial reuse until:

    the two mechanical corrections have a pass-specific Nsight A/B;
    the UPT hot receiver ABI is explicitly admitted in the GPU work ledger;
    the reservoir hot/cold decision is recorded;
    projected temporal and spatial bytes/pixel are bounded before coding;
    and the measured counters no longer show a memory ceiling with idle RT
        capacity under the accepted workload.
