Stability result
----------------

The UPT queue submit spans only 4.67 to 4.83 ms across all four captures:

    mean                         4.77 ms
    maximum range               0.16 ms
    maximum range / mean        3.35 percent

The three material UPT dispatch ranges span only 4.24 to 4.37 ms in total:

    mean                         4.3225 ms
    maximum range               0.13 ms
    maximum range / mean        3.01 percent

They account for 90.36 to 90.85 percent of the measured submit. The unlabeled
remainder is nearly fixed at 0.43 to 0.46 ms. This capture set therefore does
not reproduce a growing allocation, barrier, descriptor, or pipeline-creation
cost in the UPT submit.

Individual marker time does move between stages. The frozen capture moves
about 0.4 ms into shared-primary relative to the other three. The live/-1
capture moves about 0.3 ms from the resolve/other distribution into D0. Those
movements mostly cancel at submit scope. It would be incorrect to diagnose a
D0 regression from the single 1.62 ms sample while ignoring that its whole
submit is within 0.06 ms of the four-capture mean.

Performance interpretation
--------------------------

At this no-reuse baseline, the largest repeatable GPU item is not a UPT
reservoir or resolve operation. It is the shared P0 primary-surface ray trace
at roughly 2.4 ms in three of four captures. D0 is normally about 1.3 ms and
the trace-free R0 resolve is 0.3 to 0.6 ms. Bindings and barriers do not explain
the cost.

The elapsed-time result alone appeared to be evidence against a broad
low-level multiplier inside the new UPT producer/resolve lane on this simple
scene. The later live-SPIR-V and counter audit in 18 supersedes that efficiency
interpretation: P0 writes unused RR guides, unified D0 loads a 176-byte receiver
twice, and P0+D0+R0 move at least 716 logical bytes/pixel before AS, geometry,
material, light, and texture traffic. Stable time does not make that design
acceptable. It is not yet an equal-work NVIDIA reference comparison. The next
useful comparison remains pass-specific:

    compare D0 against the matching no-reuse RTXDI/PT initial sampler
    compare R0 against a trace-free packed-reservoir resolve
    treat P0 separately because it is shared upstream work

Do not compare only whole-frame FPS between the two applications. Record
resolution, ray policy, material set, candidate count, and dispatch dimensions
beside each corresponding marker.

FullSample Vulkan comparison captures
-------------------------------------

The three newest FullSample captures supplied after the rbdoom capture set are:

    FullSample_2026_08_02_17_57_29.ngfx-gputrace
    FullSample_2026_08_02_17_58_02.ngfx-gputrace
    FullSample_2026_08_02_17_58_18.ngfx-gputrace

All three dispatch at 3014 x 1761, or 5,307,654 pixels. That is 1.439793 times
the pixel count of the 2560 x 1440 UPT captures, so they do not satisfy the
formal identical-resolution gate. The middle capture is unambiguously the
empty view from its 0.23 ms G-buffer and 1.63 ms initial-sample cost. The last
is the busy view. The first is retained as a second populated view.

The direct-lighting path is shared by NVIDIA's renderer modes. Therefore
DIGenerateInitialSamples and DIShadeSamples are valid comparison ranges for
the direct component even though no dedicated PT-prefixed DI marker exists.
They must not, however, be represented as the complete unified DI+GI PT path.

The FullSample G-buffer is rasterized. In the busy capture, GBufferFill owns a
vkCmdDraw range of 0.97 ms within its 1.29 ms marker. It is not an equal-work
comparison for UPT.P0 SharedPrimary DispatchRays, which is ray traced. Do not
use the roughly 0.8-0.9 ms normalized FullSample G-buffer number to diagnose a
primary-ray multiplier.

The shared DI comparison is still informative. UPT's unified D0 dispatch is
1.27-1.62 ms and its trace-free R0 resolve is 0.30-0.62 ms at native 1440p.
Their measured 1.57-1.95 ms combined range is below the populated FullSample
direct-only initial+shade normalized range of 3.57-3.95 ms. The first standard
capture supplies the widest UPT value because its resolve is 0.62 ms; the
frozen/live pair is 1.57-1.92 ms. UPT also carries continuation and
secondary-NEE candidate work,
so these captures provide no evidence of the old three-times multiplier in the
new producer/resolve lane.

The empty FullSample view is an important workload result: at unchanged
resolution, DIGenerateInitialSamples falls from 3.89-4.27 ms in populated views
to 1.63 ms. Its cost is strongly scene/sample dependent rather than a fixed
full-screen dispatch tax. The busy-view DIShadeSamples increase to 1.80 ms is
likewise consistent with more surviving visibility/shading work.

This comparison does not close the formal equal-work gate because the
resolution, scene, primary-visibility method, and exact candidate/ray counts do
not match. It closes only the elapsed-time question raised by these captures:
the new UPT D0/R0 ranges are not slower than FullSample's shared DI ranges in
these measured views. It does not close memory efficiency or RT-core occupancy;
those are explicitly reopened by 18.
