UPT-10 Monolithic-First Unified DI/GI Optimization Plan
=======================================================

Date: 2026-08-09

Purpose
-------

Reduce the cost of the working one-continuation unified DI/GI renderer without
prematurely replacing its monolithic dispatches. ReSTIR PT Enhanced unifies the
estimator, reservoir, temporal history, spatial history, and final shading. It
does not require all work to reside in one shader, but neither does it justify
a split until redundant work inside the current monolithic implementation has
been removed and remeasured.

The accepted functional baseline must remain unchanged:

    one shared direct/global reservoir stream;
    direct k=2 and indirect k>=3 path candidates in one outer RIS;
    exact current emissive CDF and D0 previous-best reseed;
    compact32 receiver and cold history sidecar;
    proposal-parity, target-PDF-parity, and duplication contracts;
    closest-hit continuation traversal and alpha-aware visibility;
    no per-frame full-buffer clear;
    bounded existing D0/T0/S0 ray ceilings;
    Vulkan only.

Measured starting point
-----------------------

At 2560x1440 with temporal and spatial disabled, the supplied Nsight captures
reported:

    proposal parity 1, family 0 unified        29.86 ms
    proposal parity 1, family 1 direct-only     5.12 ms
    proposal parity 1, family 2 indirect-only  11.77 ms
    proposal parity 0, family 0 unified        29.96 ms
    proposal parity 0, family 1 direct-only    14.38 ms
    proposal parity 0, family 2 indirect-only  14.38 ms

The parity-1 unified shader is 12.97 ms slower than its two specialized family
times combined. Shader-source sampling attributes roughly 60.67 percent of
stalls to No Instructions, 12.44 percent to Long Scoreboard, and 12.09 percent
to Wait. This proves a serious current shader problem, but does not prove that
direct and indirect execution must be split.

The reference paper's approximately 2.74x average cost-reduction result is
cumulative. For the present one-continuation renderer, the relevant structural
gain is the removal of a separate ReSTIR DI initial/temporal/spatial/shading
chain. Initial-only Russian roulette is deferred until multiple bounces exist.

Primary hypothesis
------------------

The current unified shaders repeat receiver preparation and retain more
material/candidate state than necessary inside the light-candidate loops.
The strongest concrete instance is secondary NEE: every candidate currently
reloads the same material and can resample the same diffuse and specular
textures. The material is invariant for all light proposals at that secondary
hit. The RTXDI path-tracer bridge instead resolves a secondary RAB surface once
and reuses that prepared surface for its complete NEE schedule.

The first optimization family therefore removes repeated work while retaining
the monolithic dispatch and exact estimator. Dispatch splitting, work queues,
and compacted replay lists remain later evidence-gated alternatives.

Ordered work
------------

UPT-10A - Prepared direct receiver closure

1. Add a transport-facing prepared-direct-material value to the existing
   material-provider contract.
2. Resolve and validate the primary receiver once before the direct RIS loop.
3. Resolve the secondary material and its texture inputs once after the
   continuation hit and before the secondary NEE RIS loop.
4. Evaluate only direction- and radiance-dependent OpenPBR terms per light
   candidate.
5. Keep exact RGB contribution, scalar target, directional PDF, lobe mask,
   MIS, UCW conversion, visibility selection, and reservoir ordering
   unchanged.
6. Preserve the old evaluator entry points as wrappers for temporal, spatial,
   diagnostic, and deterministic callers until each is migrated deliberately.

Static gates:

    focused UPT-04 shader build and verification pass;
    UPT-04 CPU contract harness passes unchanged;
    64-byte reservoir and compact32 receiver reflection unchanged;
    RayQuery count and D0 maximum ray ceiling unchanged;
    no new descriptor, buffer, clear, barrier, or dispatch;
    production shader closes over the same light/material resources.

Runtime gates:

    fixed-sample family-0 image has no energy or identity change;
    emissive, analytic, endpoint, and secondary NEE debug views agree;
    mirror geometry/material and alpha-cutout regression scenes remain fixed;
    family 0/1/2 parity-1 and parity-0 D0 markers are recaptured;
    shader-source stalls and live-register count are recorded.

UPT-10B - Candidate live-range and repeated-math reduction

Only after 10A evidence:

1. Separate immutable receiver closure from selected-candidate state so the
   full proposal record is not kept live across unrelated work.
2. Hoist light-range, candidate-count, proposal-mixture, and material-invariant
   OpenPBR terms out of loops where static inspection proves equivalence.
3. Replace avoidable branches with selection operations only when generated
   SPIR-V and fixed-sample output prove the change.
4. Keep pHat = luminance/max of the exact F convention selected by the active
   parity policy. Do not invent a cheaper approximate target in this slice.
5. Keep selected-only visibility and never add visibility to every candidate.

UPT-10C - Production specialization cleanup

1. Confirm family specialization removes unreachable direct or indirect code
   from family 1 and family 2 blobs.
2. Remove production reachability of diagnostics, proof-only branches, and
   inactive policy implementations where specialization constants already fix
   their values.
3. Compare blob size, instruction count, register attribution, and pass time.
4. Do not remove accepted proposal-parity, compact32, duplication, or replay
   variants; make them compile-time-specialized rather than dynamically live.

UPT-10D - Temporal and spatial prepared replay

After D0 is accepted, apply the same invariant-work rule independently to T0
and S0:

    prepare each current/history receiver once;
    resolve each replayed secondary material once per shifted path;
    do not repeat texture decode for the inner light schedule;
    retain current-domain target evaluation and exact shift/Jacobian rules;
    retain deferred BASIC reciprocal replay where selection allows it;
    preserve rejection rather than admitting unsupported history.

Each pass receives its own fixed-sample, energy, rejection-diagnostic, and
Nsight gate. A D0 improvement does not authorize an unmeasured T0/S0 rewrite.

UPT-10E - Monolithic acceptance or split escalation

Re-evaluate the monolithic renderer only after 10A through 10D have removed
proven redundant work. Keep it when:

    unified time is close to shared setup plus useful direct/indirect work;
    active threads, register pressure, and instruction availability are sane;
    no full-frame intermediate traffic is needed;
    fixed-sample output and estimator statistics remain exact.

Escalate to a physical split only if the cleaned monolithic shader still shows
an intrinsic fusion penalty such as instruction-cache pressure, excessive
register allocation, or path-class divergence. First remeasure the existing
r_pathTracingUnifiedPtSplitInitial implementation. Any accepted split must:

    retain one outer unified reservoir, not separate DI and GI reservoirs;
    preserve candidate order, M, weight sum, random dimensions, and MIS;
    include intermediate barriers and traffic in its timing;
    win on the complete D0a + barrier + D0b marker sum;
    avoid a new full-frame queue unless compacted useful work repays it.

Deferred work
-------------

The following are not part of the current one-bounce optimization sequence:

    multi-bounce transport;
    initial-only Russian roulette;
    forced NEE reconnection for long replay paths;
    generic wavefront path tracing;
    approximate target functions;
    broader portal/light filtering;
    extra random candidates;
    denoiser compensation.

Build, deployment, and checkpoint
---------------------------------

Build from neo:

    cmake --build --preset win64-pt-dev-release

Deploy only:

    build-win64-pt-dev/Release/RBDoom3BFG.exe
        -> E:/prog/rbdoom-3-BFG-prebuilt/RBDoom3BFG.exe

and Vulkan blobs actually rebuilt under:

    base/renderprogs2/spirv/

Do not build DX12 shaders. Preserve unrelated dirty work. Commit one checkpoint
after a meaningful slice has passed static/build gates and, where available,
runtime validation. Document measured results here, in the owning pass document,
and in 23_handover_2026_08_08.txt.

UPT-10A build checkpoint, 2026-08-09
------------------------------------

The first prepared-closure slice is implemented without changing a persistent
buffer, descriptor, dispatch, barrier, candidate count, random dimension, ray
ceiling, reservoir formula, or visibility rule.

The material-provider boundary now exposes a local prepared-direct-material
value. D0 prepares the primary receiver once before previous-best plus fresh
direct RIS. After a continuation hit, it resolves the secondary Doom material,
diffuse/specular textures, YCoCg conversion, GUI vertex-color policy, normal,
view direction, roughness, and F0 once before the secondary NEE loop. Each
candidate retains the exact direction-dependent OpenPBR evaluation, RGB F,
target, directional PDF, MIS, and inner-RIS UCW conversion. Existing unprepared
entry points remain wrappers for callers not yet migrated.

Verified gates:

    PathTraceSlangUpt04BackendClosure                 pass
    PathTraceSlangUpt07TemporalOwnership              pass
    cmake --build --preset win64-pt-dev-release       pass
    UPT-04 static family/material/repeatability gates pass
    UPT-07 temporal artifact gate                     pass
    UPT-09 spatial artifact gate                      pass
    PathTraceUnifiedPrimaryScheduleHarness            pass
    PathTraceUnifiedTemporalContractHarness           pass

The main compact32/geometry48/light64 D0 blob changed from 646,408 to
640,616 bytes, a 5,792-byte reduction. Its Material48 and continuation32
variants show the same 5,792-byte reduction. The temporal unified base artifact
is 923,084 bytes versus the previously reported 925,792-byte runtime blob;
T0 still uses its legacy evaluation call sites, so this is not yet a temporal
performance claim. Reflection and generated verification retained the accepted
64-byte reservoir, compact32 receiver, 48-byte material, and binding contracts.

Runtime image, energy, family timing, and Nsight stall acceptance remain open.
Do not advance to 10B from build evidence alone if the deployed fixed-sample
image changes or if family-0 timing regresses.

UPT-10A runtime result and UPT-10B build checkpoint, 2026-08-09
----------------------------------------------------------------

The deployed 10A build produced no observable change at all in Nsight. Treat
prepared material closure as a successful ownership/code-size cleanup, not as
the dominant performance fix. The user did not report an image regression, so
10B was authorized; no timing win is attributed to 10A.

The first 10B slice now builds one immutable current-light schedule per direct
or secondary receiver. Candidate count, emissive/analytic domain trial counts,
proposal parity, and configured emissive trials are no longer reconstructed
inside each proposal evaluation. The exact trial order, random keys, identity
PDF, target, source PDF, fixed-M denominator, duplicate rejection, and
selected-only visibility policy remain unchanged.

Candidate winner state no longer retains a complete 32-byte trace request.
Each valid proposal retains only its direction and distance; the accepted
primary visibility request is materialized once after RIS selection. Stable
identity words and event kind are derived from the selected current light and
type rather than carried through every loop iteration. Eligibility is still
checked before reservoir admission, so this does not admit a sample whose ray
request would be invalid.

One stronger replay-compaction variant removed direction/distance too and
replayed the selected triangle or analytic sphere after RIS. It compiled and
passed closure, but expanded the main production SPIR-V to 703,144 bytes.
That variant was rejected before deployment. The retained endpoint-state
variant produces:

    compact32 geometry48 light64 unified         638,056 bytes (-2,560)
    split direct compact32 geometry48 light64    275,284 bytes (-3,272)
    split indirect compact32 geometry48 light64  394,808 bytes (+784)

The deltas compare against the accepted 10A artifacts. The indirect-only size
increase means this remains a runtime A/B, not an accepted performance win.
Focused closure, the full Vulkan release preset, UPT-04/UPT-05/UPT-07/UPT-09
static gates, and primary/temporal/duplication CPU harness execution pass.
Runtime fixed-sample image and family 0/1/2 parity-1 timing are the next gate.

UPT-10B capture audit and comparison-guard trial, 2026-08-09
------------------------------------------------------------

Four new GPU Trace reports were supplied in chronological family order 0/1/2,
then family 0 with temporal and spatial cvars enabled. The report container is
not headlessly exportable with the installed Nsight replay CLI, so exact marker
durations still require the UI values or a CSV export. Embedded marker evidence
does prove that the fourth report contains D0, unified T0, and U0 Fill SampleID,
but no S0 marker. This is expected from the current host gate: UPT-09 spatial
is direct-only and `inputs.spatial` is false unless family == DirectOnly.
The fourth capture is therefore D0+T0, not D0+T0+S0.

The user's instruction-mix observation is supported by static production
SPIR-V. Before the next trial the family 0/1/2 permutations contained:

    family 0  OpIsNan 545  OpIsInf 542
    family 1  OpIsNan 244  OpIsInf 243
    family 2  OpIsNan 308  OpIsInf 305

The family-0 counts are essentially additive. The repeated source is strict
`isfinite(value) && value > 0` validation in the candidate loops, not an extra
unified-only estimator rule. NVIDIA also guards invalid final pixels, but much
of that work is selected/final-shading work rather than an inner-RIS predicate.

An exact IEEE-754 positive-finite classifier now replaces Upt04FinitePositive:

    asuint(value) - 1u < 0x7f7fffffu

Binary32 positive finite values occupy bit patterns 1 through 0x7f7fffff, so
the expression accepts the same set and rejects +0, -0, negatives, infinities,
and all NaNs. It changes no rejection policy. It replaces an inlined floating
NaN/Inf/greater-than chain and its short-circuit branches with integer subtract
and unsigned comparison.

Static results versus the retained endpoint-state 10B build:

    family 0  638,056 -> 622,600 bytes; 39,420 -> 38,148 SPIR-V ops
    family 1  275,204 -> 266,644 bytes; 16,931 -> 16,226 SPIR-V ops
    family 2  389,416 -> 382,536 bytes; 23,727 -> 23,161 SPIR-V ops

Family 0 removes 72 instances each of OpIsNan, OpIsInf, floating greater-than,
and two short-circuit selection merges. That equals family 1's 40 plus family
2's 32 removed predicate expansions. Temporal unified base also falls from
923,084 to 900,364 bytes, and spatial direct base from 438,996 to 423,892.
Focused/full Vulkan builds and the primary/temporal/duplication CPU contracts
pass. Runtime image and marker timing remain the acceptance gate.

UPT-10B runtime result, 2026-08-09
-----------------------------------

The exact positive-finite predicate and retained candidate-state cleanup made
the direct-only and indirect-only families slightly faster in isolation, but
produced no real change in the unified family. Keep the predicate because it
preserves the exact rejection set and measurably removes generated work; do
not attribute a fusion fix to it. The large family-0 penalty remains.

This result advances the next experiment to UPT-10C. The first specialization
is intentionally limited to the actually profiled compact32 receiver with the
native 112-byte light/material records, family 0, and proposal parity 1. It
statically fixes the direct+indirect family mask and proposal-parity policy.
Parity 0 and every compact-geometry/light/material or split-continuation path
retain their existing generic artifacts, so the accepted variants are not
removed and the existing parity cvar provides the runtime A/B boundary.

UPT-10C static result and UPT-10E split trial, 2026-08-09
---------------------------------------------------------

The family-0 compact32/native112 proposal-parity specialization closes and
reduces the production SPIR-V from 581,236 to 577,796 bytes (-3,440, 0.6%).
That is too small to explain the measured fusion penalty, so it remains an
exact runtime A/B rather than an accepted solution.

The existing exact shared-reservoir split previously required the compact
geometry and compact-light packing experiments, contaminating the comparison
with their pack dispatches. UPT-10E now adds native112 split artifacts for the
same compact32 receiver used by the supplied captures. D0a writes the exact
unnormalized intermediate reservoir, a UAV barrier separates it from D0b, and
D0b extends the same reservoir and performs the sole final normalization.
There is no new queue or full-frame clear.

The split-only direct shader no longer retains proof modes 1 through 6 or the
generic proof traversal wrapper. Its production path uses the same selected
visibility RayQuery as the monolith. Static native112 artifact sizes are:

    D0a split direct     253,544 bytes
    D0b split indirect   356,504 bytes
    combined artifacts  610,048 bytes
    monolithic parity1   577,796 bytes

The combined code is larger, but each dispatch has a substantially smaller
instruction working set. Runtime acceptance therefore depends strictly on
the complete D0a + intermediate barrier + D0b sum versus monolithic D0; a win
in either child marker alone is insufficient.

    PathTraceUnifiedTemporalContractHarness        pass
    PathTraceUnifiedDuplicationContractHarness     pass (silent, exit 0)

    monolithic/split ray-query sites               3 / (1 + 2)

    deployed RBDoom3BFG.exe SHA-256                5B54893346D6B294B872035982E3B08D6B9300AA0B1C499999F4C61D1AED585D

No DX12 shader was compiled or deployed. Runtime image equivalence and the
complete split-marker timing comparison remain open; do not checkpoint the
UPT-10B/10C/10E work until those gates pass.

UPT-10E runtime result and D0b native-light load trial, 2026-08-09
------------------------------------------------------------------

The complete native112 split saved approximately 8 ms relative to the
monolithic family-0 dispatch, including D0a, the intermediate UAV barrier, and
D0b. This is a material end-to-end win and accepts the physical split as the
current production direction. The indirect D0b shader remains dominated by No
Instructions. Nsight attributed 4.13 percent of sampled input dependency to:

    OpLoad %Upt04UnifiedLightRecord_natural

The instruction eagerly loaded nearly the complete 112-byte manager record in
the secondary-NEE candidate loop. Under proposal parity 1, one emissive trial
is followed by many analytic trials, but those analytic trials still paid for
emissive-only replay identity, normal/area, and source-weight fields.

A first field-member source rewrite was rejected: Slang retained the same
whole-structure OpLoad and enlarged D0b by 320 bytes. The retained experiment
instead gives only the native112 split-indirect specialization a uint4 view of
the unchanged manager buffer. Its seven 16-byte chunks exactly cover one
112-byte record. Analytic trials load chunks 0, 2, 4, and 3 (64 bytes), while
the single emissive trial additionally loads the triangle identity/geometry
chunks it actually consumes (96 bytes). Selected fingerprint and visibility
reads use only their two required chunks.

Static result:

    whole Upt04UnifiedLightRecord loads       5 -> 0
    D0b native split SPIR-V bytes        356,504 -> 356,180
    buffer, descriptor slot, bytes and ABI    unchanged

Runtime image equivalence and the D0b marker/Nsight dependency change remain
the acceptance gate for this load-view experiment.
