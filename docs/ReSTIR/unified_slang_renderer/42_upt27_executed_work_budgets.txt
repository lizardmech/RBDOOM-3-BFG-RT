UPT-27: executed-work budgets
================================

Purpose
-------

Make hidden multiplicative GPU work observable before another emitter, route,
or material change can silently turn one logical temporal sample into many
dependent searches, loads, or traces.  This is a guard, not an estimator change
and not a performance mode.

The first admitted slice covers the currently dominant T0 unified indirect
route.  D0 and S0 retain their existing compiled ray-site ceilings; they should
adopt the same runtime counter ABI when either becomes the measured bottleneck.

Two different checks are required
---------------------------------

Compiled structure can fail the build.  VerifyUpt07TemporalDirect.cmake now
checks the emitted Vulkan SPIR-V and source closure for:

* exactly four statically bounded RayQuery initializers in the complete T0
  work-budget specialization;
* no TraceRay instruction;
* no serialized emissive-CDF binary search in temporal replay;
* exactly one declared reverse emissive-hash call path (one declaration plus
  one call in source);
* binding 31 for the per-pixel result; and
* the declared ray/remap/hash/light-load ceilings.

Executed work is scene dependent and cannot honestly fail an ordinary shader
compile.  Probe 12 executes one deterministic diagnostic T0 frame, writes one
nine-dword record per pixel, copies it asynchronously, and prints PASS/FAIL plus
mean, p50, p90, p99, maximum and active-pixel counts.  No global atomics and no
per-frame full-buffer clear are used: each valid dispatch thread owns and
initializes only its own record.

Counter ABI
-----------

Words 0--7 are logical hot-path operations:

    0 RayQuery initializations
    1 RayQuery Proceed iterations (programmable candidate pressure)
    2 previous-to-current stable remap loads
    3 reverse emissive hash-entry loads
    4 unified light-record loads
    5 geometry/material-index or full-triangle resolves
    6 material-record loads on programmable traversal/secondary evaluation
    7 texture samples on programmable alpha traversal

Word 8 is the declared-ceiling violation mask:

    bit 0 rays > 3 per pixel
    bit 1 stable remaps > 2 per pixel
    bit 2 emissive hash loads > 32 per pixel
    bit 3 light-record loads > 8 per pixel

Proceed iterations, geometry resolves, material loads and texture samples are
reported without fabricated universal ceilings because authored alpha coverage
can legitimately vary.  Their percentiles and maxima are the evidence needed
to identify the grate/character-style traversal explosion.  The log also sums
words 2--7 as logicalDependentLoadsPerPixel; this is a renderer-contract count,
not an attempted reconstruction of compiler OpLoad instructions.

Runtime protocol
----------------

Use the accepted production-shaped probe gates:

    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtLambertDiagnostic 0
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtCompactGeometry 0
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtCompactLights 1
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtSplitInitial 1
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtDuplication 1
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtTemporal 1
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtTemporalIndirect 1
    r_pathTracingUnifiedPtTemporalBottleneckProbe 12

After 16 valid-history warm-up frames, one steady-state frame is captured and
output appears after the delayed readback. Toggle the probe to 0 and back to 12
to rearm at a new camera pose.
As corrected by UPT-28, probe 12 is observer-only: it publishes the same merged
T0 reservoir as production while writing its per-pixel counter record. Stages
1--11 still republish D0 because their purpose is destructive cumulative timing.
Probe 12 therefore preserves temporal feedback and is the authoritative work
capture for position- or history-dependent cost.

Interpretation
--------------

* Large emissiveHashLoads proves endpoint-to-light reverse lookup remains in a
  hot route.  The normal stored-secondary-light replay should use stable remap
  plus the stored source PDF and avoid this search.
* Large proceed/geometry/material/texture values with ordinary ray counts prove
  programmable alpha traversal, not extra paths or light candidates.
* Large lightLoads with ordinary rays identifies repeated light materialization
  or replay.  Large remapLoads identifies repeated identity translation.
* A ray violation is an estimator/control-flow regression and should never be
  explained away by scene content.
