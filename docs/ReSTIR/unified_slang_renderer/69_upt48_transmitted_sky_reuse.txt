69_upt48_transmitted_sky_reuse.txt
=================================

Status
------

Vulkan implementation, release build, SPIR-V validation and deployment are
complete on 2026-08-15. Runtime image-quality acceptance is pending.

Observed failure
----------------

Window transmission was transparent to the primary glass path, but the light
arriving through the pane did not have one consistent UPT sample contract:

* D0 skipped recognized transparent surfaces inside its existing RayQuery;
* T0 and S0 used older transparent-material predicates, so some replay rays
  could terminate on a pane/decal that D0 had ignored;
* a continuation that reached a sky material evaluated the authored material
  stage emission, which is normally scalar white, instead of the directional
  renderer sky cube;
* the primary sky resolver colored beauty after compact32 publication, so the
  compact receiver consumed by D0/T0/S0 could retain white emission.

The last two points meant the directional sky color was outside the reservoir.
Temporal and spatial could only reuse a noisy white terminal sample; a late
beauty tint could not repair its target or weight.

Implemented contract
--------------------

1. Primary sky resolution now updates both the visible receiver and the
   authoritative compact32 receiver emission before D0. The compact write is
   an in-place UAV update; it adds no full-buffer copy or clear.

2. D0, T0 and S0 share the same transparent continuation classes: translucent,
   portal-window fallback, object-glass fallback, additive decal, filter decal
   and additive-white-key. These surfaces are ignored within the current
   RayQuery. Crossing them does not advance the UPT path length and does not
   consume a continuation bounce.

3. A continuation that terminates on a sky material samples the renderer-owned
   sky TextureCube using the actual continuation direction. The cube value is
   converted from sRGB and multiplied by `r_pathTracingSkyCubeBrightness`
   before the candidate is stored or cross-evaluated.

4. T0 and S0 replay sample that same directional terminal inside their normal
   shift/target evaluation. The transmitted sky contribution therefore enters
   D0 history and the accepted temporal/spatial estimator instead of escaping
   into a post-reservoir optical composite.

5. The D0 history ABI is version 15. Existing pages that stored scalar-white
   sky terminals are rejected by page generation. No per-frame buffer clear is
   added.

Cost and bounds
---------------

No ray, candidate, dispatch or reservoir-page count was increased. Transparent
continuation still uses the already-running RayQuery. One cube sample is issued
only when an indirect endpoint resolves to a sky material. The direct-only
specializations do not retain the cube descriptor. UPT04/07/09 control blocks
are now 220/172/100 bytes and indirect-capable variants expose cube bindings
31/36/36 respectively.

Verification
------------

`cmake --build --preset win64-pt-dev-release` passed from `neo/`.
`spirv-val --target-env vulkan1.2` passed for all 32 rebuilt Vulkan blobs. The
EXE and only those rebuilt Vulkan blobs were deployed to the prebuilt tree. No
DX12 shader compilation was invoked.

Runtime acceptance
------------------

Use the accepted `upt` configuration and compare a large window while moving:

    r_pathTracingSkyCubeEnvironment 1
    r_pathTracingSkyCubeBrightness 1

Require:

* transmitted sky illumination participates in T0/S0 and no longer becomes a
  separate high-frequency white field;
* moving past a window does not reveal a different lighting estimator;
* colored sky directions remain colored rather than pure white;
* glass crossing does not shorten the configured indirect path;
* opaque geometry, analytic lighting and non-sky emissive replay are unchanged.

If the result remains white, use `r_pathTracingSkyCubeProbe 1` and inspect the
existing sky-cube readiness/fallback log first. A one-pixel fallback cube is a
resource-publication failure, not a reservoir or transmission failure.
