UPT-45: native compact clear-window PSR
========================================

Purpose
-------

Rebuild the accepted clear-window primary-surface replacement behavior as a
UPT-owned Slang feature. Preserve the useful transport, classification and
guide semantics of the older clean renderer without importing that renderer's
host pass, binding layout, reservoir pages, wide receiver or feature registry.

This document supersedes the earlier UPT-45 adapter design that attempted to
dispatch the clean-DI transmission producer from the UPT route. That adapter
never reached an effective pipeline in runtime testing and exposed hidden
clean-renderer dependencies. Its u97/u98 alias and fallback-reservoir fixes are
historical diagnostics, not the architecture to complete.

Reference clarification: RTXDI Full Sample
-------------------------------------------

Nsight shows a final pass named `Glass` in RTXDI Full Sample, but that marker
is not its denoiser PSR producer. Source order is:

    G-buffer
    ReSTIR PT initial / temporal / spatial / final shading
        PSR data is recorded by the PT path and updated by reuse here
    denoiser
    opaque lighting composition
    Glass
        separate transparent forward/composition pass
    TAA or DLSS

The final Glass pass traces visible transparent layers and composites their
transmission, reflection and emission over the already lit image. The PSR
guide data consumed by PT reuse and the denoiser was generated earlier.

UPT must preserve the same ownership distinction:

    native receiver PSR before D0
        supplies the surface consumed by D0/T0/S0 and RR guides

    native clear-window appearance composition after R0
        applies front-pane transmission/reflection/overlay to the lit result

The late composition pass must never be mistaken for a replacement receiver,
and the pre-D0 receiver pass must not grow into a full transparent renderer.

Authoritative ownership
-----------------------

The UPT feature is implemented in its own Slang source and owned by the UPT
renderer. The old clean PSR files are references for externally visible
behavior only:

    eligible clear-window classification;
    reflection versus transmission lane selection;
    Fresnel/attenuation and composition convention;
    fail-closed handling of unsupported, missed or invalid hits;
    replacement depth, motion, normal, material identity and RR guides;
    history invalidation when the selected replacement route changes.

The UPT implementation must not include or bind:

    clean-DI reservoir pages or reservoir-count state;
    clean-DI light/ReGIR resources;
    clean material-feature registry or its global binding layout;
    the 176-byte clean primary-surface record as a companion or fallback;
    HLSL producer/composer entry points;
    a copied second glass classification system hidden in ReSTIR code.

The material implementation owns clear-glass classification and transport
parameters. The PSR pass owns tracing and replacement publication. D0/T0/S0
continue to see one final receiver contract and contain no glass branch.

Native compact contract
-----------------------

The producer reads the canonical UPT current receiver and history sidecar:

    Upt04PackedPrimarySurfaceRecord32             32 bytes / pixel
    Upt04PrimaryHistorySidecar                     32 bytes / pixel

It publishes the replacement directly into those same canonical UPT outputs.
No wide companion record is produced. The replacement must retain all fields
needed by:

    D0 receiver evaluation;
    T0/S0 surface validation and stable identity;
    previous-frame reprojection;
    DLSS Ray Reconstruction depth, normal, motion and material guides;
    late clear-window composition.

Ordinary opaque and rejected pixels retain the primary producer's original
compact record. A replacement is admitted only after a valid compatible hit
and complete compact/sidecar construction. Missing data fails closed; density
is never a reason to publish an invalid receiver.

Pass graph
----------

The admitted graph is:

    shared compact primary producer
    UPT.PSR ClearWindow (native Slang)
        classify the front receiver
        trace only the selected replacement route
        publish compact32 + history sidecar + RR guide facts
    UPT D0
    UPT T0
    UPT S0
    UPT R0
    UPT.GlassCompose (native Slang)
        apply front-pane transmission/reflection/overlay to R0
    optional DLSS Ray Reconstruction

The two UPT-45 entry points may share a small glass transport contract, but
neither may import D0/T0/S0 implementation modules. Composition may consume a
narrow PSR sidecar when front-pane facts cannot be represented in compact32;
such a resource requires a separate work-ledger admission and must not become
a second full primary-surface stream.

Ray and work ceiling
--------------------

The native implementation remains bounded to:

    zero PSR rays for an ineligible or unsupported primary material;
    at most one selected replacement ray for the first receiver mode;
    at most one reflection ray and one transmission ray only if the accepted
        old behavior genuinely requires both for late composition;
    no candidate, reservoir, temporal, spatial or duplication work;
    no per-frame full-buffer clears;
    no full-screen wide-record copy;
    no new light selection or emissive identity search.

An eligibility-first full-screen kernel is the first implementation. A compact
work queue is admitted only when counters prove that eligible pixels are sparse
and total queue classify/dispatch cost wins. It must reuse an existing bounded
queue where possible and never add a clear-sized-to-resolution requirement.

GPU footprint and register contract
-----------------------------------

Correct output is insufficient if the new pass recreates the latency and
occupancy failures already found in UPT. The native Slang PSR must be designed
around a narrow live working set.

Mandatory source rules:

1. Load receiver, geometry and material fields individually or through a
   purpose-built narrow PSR view. Do not return the 176-byte receiver, the
   112-byte light record, or a full OpenPBR closure into the ray loop.
2. Keep persistent identity, previous-frame facts and composition-only fields
   out of the traversal live range. Materialize them after a hit is selected.
3. Request only position, geometric/shading normal, texcoord and the glass
   parameters actually used by the selected mode. Do not decode tangent,
   emission, every texture channel or every material flag unconditionally.
4. Store compact hit identity and barycentrics across the query; reconstruct
   the output after traversal. Do not keep complete triangle/material records
   live across RayQuery.
5. Use static specializations for clear transmission and sharp reflection
   when that removes dead resources/code. Do not add runtime material-interface
   dispatch or a monolithic all-material closure.
6. Keep the eligibility reject before geometry/material expansion and before
   any ray query.

Required build evidence per entry point:

    Slang blob bytes and hash;
    SPIR-V instruction count and RayQuery-site count;
    reflected descriptor list proving no clean-DI/light/reservoir imports;
    compact record sizes and maximum declared ray count;
    dependency closure proving D0/T0/S0 modules are unchanged.

Required Nsight acceptance at 2560x1440 and matched camera positions:

    registers per thread, including maximum live register site;
    achieved occupancy and active warps;
    No Instructions and Long Scoreboard fractions;
    eligible-pixel and rays-per-pixel counters;
    pass time with no visible glass, sparse glass and glass filling the view.

The initial budget is a gate, not a guessed performance promise:

    no-glass cost must be close to one compact eligibility read per pixel;
    no full-screen traversal or material decode when eligible count is zero;
    no regression in D0/T0/S0 register count or shader binaries;
    no PSR kernel whose register pressure lowers occupancy below the accepted
        UPT pass it replaces without a measured total-frame win;
    any field or descriptor not named by the live algorithm is a build failure.

Staged implementation
---------------------

S45.0 authority correction

    Remove the clean-adapter design from the accepted contract. Record the
    Full Sample PSR-versus-final-Glass distinction and the GPU footprint gate.

S45.1 narrow contract audit

    Trace the canonical compact32/sidecar producer and every D0/T0/S0/RR
    consumer. Derive the minimum replacement receiver, material feature and
    optional composition-sidecar fields. Record exact byte and binding costs
    before adding a shader.

S45.2 native Slang receiver producer

    Add a UPT-owned Slang entry point and UPT-owned host pipeline. Implement
    eligibility-first replacement tracing and direct compact32/sidecar/RR-guide
    publication. It must run correctly with temporal and spatial disabled.

S45.3 native Slang appearance composition

    Add the separate post-R0 pass for the front-pane energy convention. Match
    the accepted old clear-glass appearance without using its host layout or
    shader entry point.

S45.4 remove rejected adapter

    Remove UPT-only clean-DI aliases, wide companion mode, fallback reservoir
    bindings and clean material-feature pipeline dependencies. Preserve clean
    renderer behavior and unrelated dirty work.

S45.5 runtime and footprint acceptance

    Validate image/history/RR behavior, counters, descriptor closure, Nsight
    register/occupancy evidence and matched timings. Default promotion is a
    separate decision after this gate passes.

Diagnostics
-----------

The native host and shader diagnostics must distinguish:

    requested / effective and every resource/pipeline gate;
    eligible primary pixels;
    unsupported material rejection;
    replacement query attempted / hit / miss;
    geometry decode and material decode failure;
    compact receiver publication;
    history-sidecar and RR-guide publication;
    late composition pixels and reflection/transmission lanes;
    exact rays issued by lane;
    descriptor and SPIR-V footprint summary.

Counters are atomics-free per-pixel or compact reductions where practical and
default off. Enabling diagnostics must not change estimator acceptance.

Acceptance
----------

1. Clear windows shade the valid replacement receiver in D0 with T0/S0 off.
2. T0/S0 introduce no front-pane ghost receiver, stale identity, corrupted
   replay, white/color error or runaway energy.
3. Late composition matches the accepted old clear-window transmission and
   sharp-reflection behavior at the same material and camera position.
4. RR guides follow the replacement surface without pane-edge smearing or
   history reset storms.
5. Opaque UPT output, bindings, shader binaries and timing are unchanged when
   UPT-45 is compiled or gated out.
6. No clean-DI reservoir, ReGIR, wide receiver or feature-registry dependency
   exists in the UPT-45 reflected binding/dependency closure.
7. No per-frame full-buffer clear or full-screen wide copy is added.
8. Nsight proves the native PSR live working set does not introduce needless
   register pressure, occupancy collapse or serialized dependent-load chains.
9. Vulkan focused and full release builds pass. No DX12 shader is compiled.
