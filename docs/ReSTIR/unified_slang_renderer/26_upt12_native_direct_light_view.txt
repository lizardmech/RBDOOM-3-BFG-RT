UPT-12 Native Direct-Light uint4 View
=====================================

Date: 2026-08-09

Decision
--------

Reject UPT-11 light tiles after no measurable Nsight change. The next bounded
slice mechanically enables the already implemented native uint4 current-light
view in direct D0. It changes neither the light universe nor the estimator.

Profiler evidence
-----------------

The advanced Nsight CSV exports separate the three live kernels:

    direct D0a:
        589,423 samples;
        63.56 sample-weighted live registers, maximum 91;
        51.34 percent of samples above 64 registers;
        one RayQueryProceed site;
        hot whole Upt04UnifiedLightRecord load:
            39,264 samples;
            129,523 dependency-attributed samples;
            7,206-cycle average warp latency;
            54-instruction mix;
            32 active threads per warp.

    indirect D0b:
        1,173,747 samples;
        80.56 sample-weighted live registers, maximum 108;
        91.19 percent of samples above 64 registers;
        two RayQueryProceed sites with 14 and 10 active threads per warp;
        no whole Upt04UnifiedLightRecord load;
        the three hot native light reads are uint4 loads.

    resolve:
        62,102 samples;
        11.98 sample-weighted live registers, maximum 17;
        no RayQuery site and no whole light-record load.

This confirms two distinct bottlenecks. Direct has one fully converged scattered
whole-record load. Indirect is primarily a large-kernel/register/traversal
divergence problem; narrowing more record loads cannot solve its 40.5 percent
No Instructions stall share.

Implementation
--------------

`upt04_initial_bindings.slang` already owns the exact native112 uint4 view and
field-specific helpers:

    evaluation loads only type-dependent chunks;
    selected identity loads chunks 4 and 5;
    visibility ignore loads chunks 4 and 5;
    complete replay loads all seven chunks only when the complete record is
    actually required.

UPT-12 enables `UPT04_NATIVE_LIGHT_UINT4_VIEW=1` for:

    upt04_initial_split_direct_rayquery_compact32;
    upt04_initial_direct_only_rayquery_compact32;
    the rejected opt-in light-tile direct variant for ABI consistency.

No shader logic, candidate count, random dimension, proposal/target PDF,
reservoir convention, visibility rule, ray query, descriptor, or dispatch is
changed. Native records remain authoritative and retain their 112-byte stride.

Verification
------------

The build-time UPT-12 verifier rejects any eager
`OpLoad %Upt04UnifiedLightRecord_natural` in the native direct split,
compact32 direct-only, or native indirect split artifacts. Current results:

    direct split:    whole loads 0, uint4 load sites 18, RayQuery sites 1;
    direct-only:     whole loads 0, uint4 load sites 18, RayQuery sites 1;
    indirect split:  whole loads 0, uint4 load sites 16, RayQuery sites 2.

The full Vulkan release preset and all five PathTraceUnified CPU contract tests
pass. No DX12 shader was compiled.

Runtime gate
------------

Capture the native split with `r_pathTracingUnifiedPtLightTiles 0`. Compare D0a
and the complete D0a + intermediate barrier + D0b unit against the preceding
native split. The direct capture must no longer show the hot 54-instruction
whole-record load. Accept only a measured time reduction with image parity.

If the result is again negligible, stop pursuing current-light load width. The
next evidence-backed lane is indirect D0b footprint/register reduction followed
by bounded replay/traversal compaction; do not add more light candidates or
change temporal/spatial reuse.

Runtime result
--------------

The deployed UPT-12 build produced no visible or measurable change. The native
direct-light view remains an exact, neutral load-shaping cleanup, but it is
rejected as a performance solution. Stop widening this experiment into more
current-light load work. D0b owns roughly twice the samples of D0a and its
advanced capture places 91.19 percent of samples above 64 live registers; that
is the next bounded target.
