2. The original bug - CONFIRMED, do not re-diagnose
----------------------------------------------------

A small hanging light fixture cost about 30 percent of frame time when on
screen. The indirect dispatch is roughly 60 percent of the frame. Temporal
and spatial reuse OFF throughout.

Cause: the indirect shader is one inlined Slang entry point. When a pixel
selects or hits an emissive triangle it enters a large emissive closure -
geometry replay, material record load, bindless emissive texture sample, and
a validation branch tree. That code is never fetched when no emissive
triangle is in play. When the fixture appears it becomes resident and most
of the cost lands on unrelated code as instruction-fetch stalls.

Proof, valid A/B, Nsight source captures:

    artifact                       wall        fixture      cliff     cliff %
    probe 4 production  42,904 i   701,283   1,385,588   +684,305   +97.6%
    probe 6 excised     36,306 i   860,484     963,390   +102,906   +12.0%

    newly-executed      probe 4: 1,393 instrs / +286,781 samples
                        probe 6:    76 instrs /   +6,365 samples

Only the within-artifact wall-to-fixture ratio is meaningful. Do not compare
absolute sample counts across capture sessions.

Residual after excision: the remaining +12 percent is entirely SKINNED
GEOMETRY decode - gUpt04SkinnedSourceIndices, gUpt04SkinnedCurrentVertices,
gUpt04SkinnedTriangles. Not emissive. Unscoped. Connects to a prior finding
about one merged dynamic BLAS rebuilt every frame.

Chronic problem, out of scope for UPT-56: No Instructions 63-72 percent on
the wall camera with the fixture absent, on BOTH artifacts. Gate: wall-camera
No Instructions below 15 percent. Levers: selective noinline on cold blocks,
spec constants for geometry routes, less Unroll.

D1. Packed radiance is piecewise-constant over four regions
    (Upt56ResolvedRadianceAtBarycentrics). Small fixture triangles are

    that reads standalone_emissive_closure_task.txt STATUS first will
    implement the wrong thing.

5. Measurement rules - these were learned expensively
------------------------------------------------------

R1. Verify the artifact hash and instruction count changed in the intended
    direction BEFORE capturing. Three cycles were lost to A/Bs that ran the
    production artifact while the console said otherwise.

R2. Cross-launch frame-time comparison has a measured 20-37 PERCENT NOISE
    FLOOR on this kernel, from driver register allocation differing between
    process launches with byte-identical SPIR-V. Any effect smaller than
    that is not measurable by that method. Within-session wall-to-fixture
    ratios are protected and remain valid.

R3. Report absolute stall counts, never shares alone. A share moves when
    either term moves, and the causes are opposite.

R4. A zero-sample region means "not executed in this configuration", not
    "dead code". The x3 lane looked dead in one capture and carries 45
    percent of samples in another.

R5. Long Scoreboard is real memory work; No Instructions is overhead. If
    Long Scoreboard is flat or negative while totals rise, the cost is not
    traversal or memory.

R6. Frame time is reported last, never alone. Three consecutive experiments
    in this project were accepted or rejected on it and two were null
    results that concealed what happened.

R7. cvar changes do not necessarily reach the GPU. The probe cvar is absent
    from EnsurePipeline's invalidation list and has no CVAR_ARCHIVE, so it
    must be set with +set at launch. Verify from the capture, not the
    console.
