Basic Glass PSR Reflection Contract
===================================

Status
------

Implemented and runtime validated. This document describes the clear-glass
transport portion of the single production reflection system. The authoritative
whole-system description is stable_reflection_hit_lighting_steps.txt.


Per-Pixel Flow
--------------

    original glass surface
        -> thin-glass Fresnel/refraction payload
        -> deterministic mirror hit
        -> exact reflected-hit emissive
        -> bounded analytic RIS direct shade
        -> reflection sidecar
        -> behind-glass PSR resolved surface
        -> existing clean DI/GI
        -> dense RR material guides + mirror hit distance
        -> late T + R compose

The producer traces the mirror hit for every eligible glass pixel so reflection
material detail and directly hit emissive are dense. It does not choose sparse
reflection/transmission pixels.


Transport Rules
---------------

    one mirror ray per eligible glass pixel
    one behind-glass/refraction ray per eligible glass pixel
    deterministic/sticky strong-Fresnel integration-surface ownership
    no stochastic lobe sampling
    no throughput/selection-PDF estimator
    no mirror-secondary surface buffer
    no linked reflection GI
    no reflection reservoir/history

When reflection owns the primary integration surface, existing clean DI shades
that resolved hit and the private reflection shade is skipped. Otherwise the
behind-glass surface owns clean DI and the reflection sidecar supplies the dense
non-owning reflection lobe.


Hit-Lighting Rules
------------------

The reflection shade begins with:

    max(reflectionHit.material.emissiveRadiance, 0)

It then performs M analytic proposals and commits at most one direct shade and
one shadow ray. Emissive is therefore exact and independent of RIS.

Default budget:

    r_pathTracingReflectionSecondarySamples 8
    r_pathTracingReflectionSecondaryShadows 1


Sidecar Contract
----------------

Reflection sidecar alpha states:

    0.0     empty
    0.125   rejected reflection candidate
    0.25    mirror miss
    0.375   candidate/debug
    0.5     transmission-selected PSR metadata
    0.875   dense hybrid reflection radiance
    1.0     reflection-selected resolved primary

The 0.875 payload is Fresnel-weighted reflection radiance. Compose adds it once.
The 1.0 payload is reflection throughput for a reflected surface already shaded
by clean DI. The two states must never be interpreted interchangeably.


RR Rules
--------

Clear glass keeps behind-glass geometry guides while blending deterministic
reflected-hit material detail into albedo/specular guides. The actual mirror ray
length is exported as specular hit distance. Emissive chromaticity is preserved
in the specular guide.

Do not place reflected radiance itself into material guide channels.


Production Defaults
-------------------

    r_pathTracingCleanRtxdiDiGlassReflectionPsr 1
    r_pathTracingCleanRtxdiDiTransmissionProducer 1
    r_pathTracingCleanRtxdiDiTransmissionCompose 1
    r_pathTracingCleanRtxdiDiGlassShader 1
    r_pathTracingReflectionSecondarySamples 8
    r_pathTracingReflectionSecondaryShadows 1


Validation
----------

Beauty:

    head-on glass retains transmission plus weak reflection
    grazing glass increases reflection
    reflected emissives are solid
    analytic lighting is present and shadowed
    no gray proxy appears when a reflection is dark

RR:

    albedo/specular detail is dense
    hit distance follows mirror geometry
    DLSS-RR reconstructs the reflection
    stationary and moving views do not show stochastic lobe holes

Safety:

    no reflection-only reservoir allocation
    no mirror-secondary full-screen buffer
    no linked GI reflection dispatch
    no stale sidecar signal after producer disable/re-enable
