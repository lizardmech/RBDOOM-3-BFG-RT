Reflection Secondary Trace and Shade Service
============================================

Status
------

Implemented and runtime validated as part of the single dense clear-glass
reflection system. This module is a reusable service, not a second renderer.


Responsibilities
----------------

pathtrace_reflection_secondary.hlsli owns:

    mirror direction construction
    one exact reflection trace
    resolved hit payload
    exact hit emissive
    Doom analytic light decoding
    M-candidate streaming RIS selection
    at most one selected analytic shade
    optional one selected-light visibility ray

It does not own:

    glass Fresnel or transmission
    PSR surface publication
    reflection sidecar composition
    RR guide policy
    reservoir allocation or history
    GI
    emissive-light sampling


Trace Contract
--------------

Input:

    resolved caller surface
    valid reflection direction/candidate

Output:

    valid flag
    hit distance
    hit position
    ray direction
    hit payload

The clear-glass caller uses a perfect mirror direction. Future glossy callers
may supply a VNDF-sampled direction but must reuse this service rather than
creating another reflection pipeline.


Shade Contract
--------------

The shade starts with exact material emission:

    radiance = max(hitSurface.material.emissiveRadiance, 0)

Analytic direct lighting then uses:

    candidateCount = r_pathTracingReflectionSecondarySamples (default 8)
    proposal domain = current Doom analytic lights
    zero-target rejection
    streaming RIS selection
    one selected analytic light sample
    optional selected-light shadow ray

The returned radiance is unweighted by glass Fresnel. The caller multiplies by
the glass reflection throughput exactly once before writing the sidecar.


Cost Contract
-------------

Per valid transmission-owned glass pixel:

    one mirror ray
    M cheap analytic proposals
    at most one analytic light shade
    at most one shadow ray

M is not multi-SPP. Raising M changes proposal selection quality without adding
multiple committed light shades.


Controls
--------

    r_pathTracingReflectionSecondarySamples     default 8, range 1..16
    r_pathTracingReflectionSecondaryShadows     default 1

There are no alternate lighting modes, linked DI/GI controls, reflection cache
controls, or reflection reservoir controls.


Callers
-------

Current production caller:

    pathtrace_clean_rtxdi_di_transmission_producer.hlsli

Future permitted callers:

    explicit opaque near-mirror material feature
    narrow GGX/VNDF glossy feature within a measured roughness limit

Future callers must share this implementation and its sidecar/guide ownership;
they must not clone the trace, shade, or light-selection system.


Acceptance
----------

Validated clear-glass behavior:

    solid directly hit emissive
    analytic direct lighting at M=8
    selected-light shadows
    dense RR albedo/specular material detail
    working DLSS-RR reconstruction
    no reflection history or duplicate reservoir pipeline
