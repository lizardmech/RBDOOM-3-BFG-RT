LPD-00F FIXTURE COMMAND MANIFEST
================================

Status: commands are implemented and built, but the map/getviewpos and positive
captures below remain pending supervisor/manual runtime acceptance.

Candidate material declarations
-------------------------------

Liquid:
  textures/decals/splat7

Ordinary filter negative control:
  textures/decals/stainwall

The command performs exact declaration lookup and rejects wildcard, extension,
traversal, drive-path, unknown, and case-mismatched inputs.

Common setup (pending fixed interactive location)
-------------------------------------------------

  devmap game/mars_city1
  setviewpos <accepted-x> <accepted-y> <accepted-z> <accepted-yaw>
  getviewpos
  r_pathTracingCrosshairMaterialDump 1

Aim at a broad opaque concrete/stone receiver. For B8, aim within 48 units of an
opaque perpendicular plane. The command rejects translucent, glass-surface-type,
area-portal, no-impact, no-hit, and degenerate-normal receivers.

B3: two identical aligned cards
--------------------------------

  liquidPoolFixture aligned 2 textures/decals/splat7
  screenshot screenshots/liquid_pool_B3.png
  conDump liquid_pool_B3

B4: four identical aligned cards
---------------------------------

  liquidPoolFixture aligned 4 textures/decals/splat7
  screenshot screenshots/liquid_pool_B4.png
  conDump liquid_pool_B4

B5: two partially overlapping cards
-------------------------------------

  liquidPoolFixture partial 2 16 textures/decals/splat7
  screenshot screenshots/liquid_pool_B5.png
  conDump liquid_pool_B5

The second footprint is exactly 16 world units along the printed deterministic
receiver tangent. All shifted centers must retrace to the same entity, material,
and plane before either decal is emitted.

B6: deliberately over-capacity fixture
---------------------------------------

  liquidPoolFixture aligned 32 textures/decals/splat7
  screenshot screenshots/liquid_pool_B6.png
  conDump liquid_pool_B6

32 is the documented safe developer maximum and is intentionally above the
current generic three-entry bin. Replace 32 with accepted plannedCapacity+1 only
after LPD-04 freezes a lower capacity; never exceed 32.

B8: nearby incompatible receiver planes
----------------------------------------

  liquidPoolFixture perpendicular textures/decals/splat7
  screenshot screenshots/liquid_pool_B8.png
  conDump liquid_pool_B8

The command first validates the crosshair receiver and probes four deterministic
tangent directions for the nearest safe plane whose absolute normal dot is <=
0.25 within 48 units. It emits nothing unless both receivers are found.

Mixed liquid/filter receiver-retrace control
--------------------------------------------

  liquidPoolFixture mixedFilter textures/decals/splat7 textures/decals/stainwall
  screenshot screenshots/liquid_pool_mixed_filter.png
  conDump liquid_pool_mixed_filter

Reset/repeat procedure (not yet accepted)
-----------------------------------------

The packet requests:
  restartMap
  setviewpos <same accepted values>
  repeat command and compare every printed input plus 16-hex-digit serial

Live source shows restartMap is a normal-mode no-op unless g_demoMode is true.
Until that is separately fixed or waived, use of `devmap game/mars_city1` as the
reset is only a proposed substitute and cannot be credited as accepted proof.

Required remaining captures
---------------------------

For one accepted map/getviewpos, capture one, aligned 2, aligned 4, partial 2,
aligned plannedCapacity+1, perpendicular, and mixedFilter. Repeat each after the
accepted reset and compare printed origin, normals, direction, size/depth/angle,
material names, count/offset, tangent, receiver identity, and fixture serial.
Crosshair material dumps must identify the requested exact declarations.
