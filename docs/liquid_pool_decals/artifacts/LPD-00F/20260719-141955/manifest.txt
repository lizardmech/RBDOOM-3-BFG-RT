Implementation correction
-------------------------

The original fixture used the rendered view and inherited view bob. A first
correction used GetEyePosition plus viewAngles/gravity/nodal offsets, but the
player eye height could still settle by about 0.018 world units after teleport
or map load. The final implementation retains that no-bob/no-kick trace basis,
then canonicalizes the two receiver-plane coordinates to a 0.125-unit grid
while retaining the collision plane distance. This removes sub-grid player
settling without moving the fixture off the contacted plane.

Final printed contract includes:
  traceBasis=noBobEyeViewAngles
  originGrid=0.125000
