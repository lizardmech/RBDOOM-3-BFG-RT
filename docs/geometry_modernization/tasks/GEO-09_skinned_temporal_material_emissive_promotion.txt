GEO-09 - Promote skinned traversal with temporal/material/emissive parity
==========================================================================

GOAL
----
Move eligible skinned geometry fully onto canonical per-instance traversal only
after every downstream consumer matches the legacy path.

PRECONDITIONS
-------------
- GEO-08 per-instance BLAS compare mode passes.
- This ticket owns consumer migration, not new lighting algorithms.

FILES TO AUDIT FIRST
--------------------
  neo/renderer/NVRHI/PathTraceSceneInputs.h
  neo/renderer/NVRHI/PathTraceSmokeSceneBuild.cpp
  neo/renderer/NVRHI/PathTraceSmokeDispatch.cpp
  neo/renderer/NVRHI/PathTracePrimarySurface.h
  neo/renderer/NVRHI/PathTraceMaterialUniverse.cpp/.h
  neo/renderer/NVRHI/PathTraceDynamicMaterialState.cpp/.h
  neo/renderer/NVRHI/PathTraceEmissiveCandidates.cpp/.h
  neo/renderer/NVRHI/PathTraceUnifiedLight.cpp/.h
  neo/shaders/builtin/pathtracing/PathTracePrimarySurface.hlsli
  neo/shaders/builtin/pathtracing/pathtrace_smoke_rab_motion_supplier.hlsli

STEPS
-----
1. Inventory all consumers of legacy merged skinned primitive/instance IDs,
   previous positions, material indices, triangle flags, and emissive records.
2. Make canonical hit routing recover mesh key, instance key, local primitive,
   current/previous position, material ID, and triangle class without depending
   on legacy merged offsets.
3. Compare primary hit tuple old/new for sampled pixels across all baseline
   models: instance, primitive, barycentrics, world position, normal/tangent/UV,
   material, and triangle flags.
4. Compare motion output for idle, animated, locomoting, newly spawned,
   teleported, offscreen-returning, and removed instances. Validate history reset
   rather than accepting plausible-looking denoiser output.
5. Build emissive records from canonical mesh + local primitive + instance
   identity. Compare current/previous inventories and remaps across animation,
   spawn/despawn, and identical model instances.
6. Prove material texture bindings and dynamic material state are identical.
   Keep Fatzombie's known NVRHI texture-descriptor rejection classified as a
   separate material defect; geometry/material identity still must match.
7. Compare DI/GI/RR geometry inputs numerically where available. Do not tune or
   replace ReSTIR/RR algorithms in this ticket.
8. Promote only surfaces whose full consumer funnel passes. Leave unsupported or
   failed surfaces on the explicit legacy fallback with counters.
9. Run crowded, static, comm1, lifecycle, PDA, and map-transition regressions.

VALIDATION
----------
- Same-frame old/new hit tuple and material/triangle identities agree.
- Current/previous emissive identity/remap has no collision or unexplained loss.
- Motion vectors/history validity agree within declared tolerance.
- No new T-pose, missing texture beyond known Fatzombie issue, light popping,
  ghosting, or unexplained reservoir reset attributable to routing.

DONE WHEN
---------
Eligible skinned geometry no longer needs the merged route for traversal,
materials, motion, or emissives; fallback remains explicit and reversible.
