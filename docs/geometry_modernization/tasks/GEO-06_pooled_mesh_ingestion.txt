GEO-06 - Ingest pooled meshes and prove shared-rigid traversal
================================================================

GOAL
----
Store canonical immutable mesh source data once per mesh in growable buffers,
prove non-zero-offset BLAS consumption, then drive a default-off canonical rigid
compare route. This gives GEO-13 a verified rigid route to promote.

PRECONDITIONS
-------------
- GEO-05 registry identity and lifetimes pass in shadow mode.
- The pool starts in shadow mode. Live rigid traversal stays default off and
  compare-capable.

STEPS
-----
1. Freeze the full-fidelity source layout: positions plus all attributes,
   topology, material IDs, triangle class/flags, and deformation metadata needed
   by current consumers. Compression is deferred to GEO-12.
2. Implement append/allocate records with 64-bit checked byte offsets, sizes,
   alignment, storage generation, and source hash/version. Reject overflow with
   a named counter; do not truncate.
3. Upload a mesh only on first residency or source revision. Make immutable
   steady-state upload bytes zero and count reuse explicitly.
4. Add a deterministic CPU test for offset/alignment/range calculations near
   32-bit boundaries and for pool growth preserving existing records.
5. Build a Vulkan BLAS micro-test using geometry at non-zero vertex and index
   offsets. Validate primitive 0 and last primitive material/attribute lookup.
6. Add shadow comparisons for per-mesh vertex/index/triangle counts, checksums,
   material ranges, and representative decoded vertices/triangles.
7. Adapt the established rigid BLAS/instance route to consume canonical pooled
   records behind one default-off gate. One immutable mesh BLAS is shared by all
   rigid instances of that mesh; transforms and instance identity stay separate.
8. In compare mode, record old/new rigid mesh and instance counts plus sampled
   hit instance/primitive/material/triangle-class tuples, current/previous
   transforms, and emissive identities. Every difference must be enumerated.
9. Exercise repeated rigid props to prove shared BLAS scaling. Exercise repeated
   zombies/Fatzombie as negative controls: their immutable source records may be
   shared, but their live output/BLAS must not enter the rigid route without the
   GEO-04/D06 single-bone proof. Rigid BLAS count scales with unique eligible
   meshes; TLAS instances scale with live rigid instances.
10. Exercise lifecycle removal/re-add and map changes. No record may reference a
   destroyed buffer generation.
11. Measure source upload, retained bytes, rigid BLAS reuse, and TLAS instances
   on identical baseline content. Do not claim a win from different routing or
   geometry counts.

GUARDRAILS
----------
- No attribute compression, meshlet conversion, static bucket cutover, or
  skinned-output promotion.
- No whole-payload hashing every steady-state frame.
- Pool growth must be generation-safe; do not overwrite in-flight buffers.

DONE WHEN
---------
- Full-fidelity records compare against legacy data.
- Non-zero-offset BLAS addressing is proven.
- Repeated instances reuse one immutable source record.
- Canonical rigid traversal matches the established rigid route for geometry,
  material, motion, emissive identity, and lifecycle under its compare gate.
- Overflow/range failures are explicit and fail closed.
