Interpret the pair as follows:

  cacheRay hit=0
    The retained vertex/index content or transform does not reproduce the
    raster receiver. Localize capture/index construction before GEO-02.

  cacheRay hit=1 and force rebuild restores the brush
    Static BLAS invalidation/reuse is the earliest failed invariant. GEO-02 may
    repair that invalidation narrowly.

  cacheRay hit=1 and force rebuild does not restore the brush
    The retained geometry is correct, but BLAS build/traversal or its TLAS
    publication remains bad. Continue instrumentation at that exact boundary.

GEO-02 remains blocked until this final A/B supplies the expected/actual
distinction. A permanent force-rebuild workaround is not authorized.
