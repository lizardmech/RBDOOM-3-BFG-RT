The route-1 implementation first performed two overlapping operations:

  1. RegisterSmokeWorldStaticMaterialTextureInfo traversed every static-world
     and rigid source model.
  2. RegisterSmokeMaterialTextureInfoForMaterialIds then hydrated the exact
     cached resident bucket material-ID set, including missing-decl discovery,
     texture-handle refresh, and residency state.

The second operation is the authoritative route-1 owner. Skipping the first
operation only when staticBucketResidentMaterialRoute is true reduces the scene
median by 5.5 ms and metadata median by 5 ms. Capture, CPU skinning, geometry
storage, descriptor layout, SBT, payloads, shader binaries, and route policy do
not change.

The remaining route-1 median difference is 4 ms. Metadata is at route-0 parity;
the difference is the required full resident-universe material table and
active-bucket emissive publication, approximately +2 ms and +2.5 ms in these
integer sub-timers. This regression is disclosed. Removing it would require a
separate cache/ownership design, not another narrow duplicate-traversal fix.
