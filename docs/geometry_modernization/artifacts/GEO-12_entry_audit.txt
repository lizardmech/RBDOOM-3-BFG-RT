GEO-12 ENTRY AUDIT: ATTRIBUTE OWNERSHIP AND MEASUREMENT SEAMS
=============================================================

DATE
----
2026-07-29

STATUS
------
Entry audit complete. The proposal is viable only with a split measurement
phase. No encoding, eligibility predicate, GPU layout, shader decode, or
default has been authorized.

The first implemented slice is observation-only and covers the value-owned
canonical rigid and skinned-bind sources. Static world brushes, generated
geometry, particles, decals, and other dynamic fallbacks are not in that
registry and remain a required second survey domain.

LIVE ATTRIBUTE CONTRACT
-----------------------
Contract / owner                         Layout             Live consumers
--------------------------------------   -----------------  ------------------
Canonical position source               float3, 12 bytes   registry, transport,
                                                            pooled source upload
Canonical immutable attribute source    20 floats, 80 B    registry, transport,
                                                            pooled source upload,
                                                            skinned-source pack
Rendered current vertex                  7 float4, 112 B    BLAS vertex stride,
                                                            all RT hit families,
                                                            emissive replay,
                                                            readback/parity
Skinned bind/input vertex                7 float4, 112 B    skinning compute
Skinned previous position                float4, 16 bytes   motion reconstruction
Primary surface record                   176 bytes          projected hit result;
                                                            no raw UV/tangent

The rendered `PathTraceSmokeVertex` is not a safe first compression seam. Its
stride is named by acceleration-structure builds and resource creation, and
the same full structure is independently declared in primary, DI, GI, debug,
smoke, and emissive shader families. Position, normal, both UV pairs, color,
color2, tangent, and bitangent are read in hit reconstruction. Changing it
would be a renderer-wide ABI migration rather than an attribute experiment.

The canonical source already has the desired first structural property:
positions are a separate float3 stream. `PtGeometrySourceAttribute` contains:

  normal float3
  diffuse texcoord float2
  color float4
  color2 float4
  tangent float3
  bitangent float3
  bitangent sign float

For skinned-bind records, color is also the byte-derived joint-index carrier
and color2 is the joint-weight carrier. Any later storage change must preserve
those semantics exactly; treating them only as display color would corrupt
skinning.

PREVIOUS POSITION AND PRIMARY-SURFACE RESULT
--------------------------------------------
GPU skinning writes current position/normal/tangent/bitangent into the rendered
vertex and writes previous position into a separate 16-byte stream. The primary
surface producer consumes the hit attributes, material result, and previous
position, then projects them into a record containing world position, geometric
and shading normals, material terms, and previous position. It does not retain
raw tangent or UV after material evaluation.

This validates the proposal's direction to keep mutable positions separate.
It does not yet answer whether skinning should write or derive normal/tangent;
that decision still requires a timed compute variant and identical decoded
surface comparisons.

SURVEY COVERAGE GAP
-------------------
`PtGeometrySourceRegistry::Observe` admits:

  RegisteredRenderModel + Rigid
  SkinnedBindSource + Skinned

It rejects or does not own:

  StaticWorldMap
  GeneratedPersistent
  UnsupportedTransient
  particles and other explicitly dynamic fallback capture

Therefore one canonical-registry capture cannot close GEO-12 and cannot supply
a universal UV predicate. Ordinary/large brush geometry, wide/repeating world
materials, live decals, emissives, particles, and small-dynamic fallbacks must
be measured at their full-fidelity capture or resident-source owner. A result
from only monsters and rigid models must not be generalized to world brushes.

FIRST BEHAVIOR-NEUTRAL SLICE
----------------------------
`PathTraceGeometryAttributeSurvey` walks the value-owned registry without
mutating, republishing, or uploading any record. It produces per-record and
aggregate measurements for:

  - position and UV min/max;
  - non-finite position, UV, basis, and color components;
  - half-UV overflow, underflow-to-zero, maximum absolute error, and maximum
    relative error;
  - degenerate normal, tangent, and bitangent counts;
  - oct16 normal and tangent maximum angular error;
  - stored-versus-derived bitangent maximum angular error and invalid count;
  - color/color2 UNORM8 exact, out-of-range, and maximum-error counts;
  - skinned joint byte integrality/range and weight component/sum ranges;
  - current source bytes and savings projected separately for each candidate.

Projected savings are deliberately independent. They are not an accepted
combined layout and they do not imply candidate eligibility.

The one-shot CVar is paged and bounded:

  r_pathTracingGeometryAttributeSurveyDump 1

Each value requests one 1-based page of at most 32 mesh records, emits the full
aggregate each time, and resets to zero. Page 2 is requested by setting the
CVar to 2, and so on. The summary reports the clamped page and page count.

The survey never clamps candidate input. Wide half values, non-finite values,
out-of-UNORM values, nonintegral joints, degenerate bases, and reconstruction
failures remain explicit counters.

WHAT THIS SLICE DOES NOT PROVE
------------------------------
- Half UV is not eligible merely because values are finite or representable.
- A maximum aggregate error is not a material-sensitive acceptance threshold.
- Oct16 normal/tangent storage is not selected.
- Stored bitangent removal is not selected.
- Exact byte-derived color in this domain does not prove all rendered dynamic
  color semantics are byte-derived.
- No source, rendered, BLAS, SBT, descriptor, or shader ABI changed.

ORDERED NEXT SLICES
-------------------
Slice 2 - complete measurement coverage

  - Add a read-only full-fidelity survey at the static-world and dynamic
    capture/resident-source owners.
  - Preserve per-surface identity/material/class in the samples.
  - Cover ordinary world, large-coordinate brushes, wide/repeating UVs,
    decals, emissives, particles, and explicit fallback classes.
  - Reconcile capture-time invalid-attribute sanitization with the unsanitized
    source values; never mistake a zeroed fallback for an eligible input.

Slice 3 - choose exactly one candidate

  - Define a numeric threshold from the combined asset/material evidence.
  - Define explicit fallback reasons and byte savings.
  - Prefer a lossless candidate if the combined survey proves one.
  - Keep the gate default off.

Slice 4 - ABI and numeric A/B

  - Add one encode/decode contract with CPU asserts and preprocessed HLSL
    review.
  - Compare decoded attributes, primary hit, UV/material result, normal-map
    basis, motion, and emissive reconstruction on identical frames.

Slice 5 - bandwidth and timing

  - Compare source and mutable bytes, uploads, skinning dispatch, BLAS work,
    and frame time on identical geometry.
  - Measure write-versus-derive normal/tangent as a separate experiment.

The first run deliberately caught and rejected a diagnostic calibration error:
the byte-derived joint value was multiplied by 255 but compared with the
pre-scale UNORM epsilon, producing false nonintegral counts. The corrected
criterion scales the epsilon by 255. The harness and `geometry_geo12b.log`
first proved zero nonintegral and zero out-of-byte components;
`geometry_geo12d.log` repeats that result on the final deployed executable.

`geometry_geo12c.log` captures pages 30 and 31. Every one of the 42 skinned
records emits its per-mesh joint/weight row with zero joint failure. The rows
include NPC heads and bodies, labcoat/suit/jumpsuit characters, and both
3,050-vertex surfaces of `models/md5/monsters/zfat/zfat.md5mesh`. This proves
the prepared crowded scene includes the intended Fatzombie source.

The page-30/31 capture preceded only a reporting hardening for wholly absent
finite joint/weight ranges. Every captured skinned row has finite ranges, so
that hardening cannot change its values. The final-build page-1 aggregate in
`geometry_geo12d.log` is otherwise identical.

The zero half-UV error is evidence about the stored canonical source values in
this capture, not proof that transformed diffuse/normal-map UVs or missing
static-world/dynamic-fallback domains are safe. No candidate is promoted.
